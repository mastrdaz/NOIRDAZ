# Noirdaz business operating system

Version 0.1 · Prepared 2026-10-13 · Proposed operating practice pending owner adoption

This is the operating reference for a Wisconsin-first software, website, hosting and managed-services business with one owner and up to five employees. It connects a lead to a signed scope, costed delivery, controlled launch, recurring support and a documented exit.

Start with [STATUS](STATUS.md), then [owner decisions](OWNER_DECISIONS.md), [client lifecycle](clients/CLIENT_LIFECYCLE.md) and the [operator guide](OPERATOR_GUIDE.md). The [brief coverage map](COVERAGE_MAP.md) identifies every requested section. Existing `../planning/wisconsin-surplus/` and `../docs/` remain unchanged; these reusable documents do not make decisions for that prospect.

## Readiness and authority

- Blank operational checklists can be copied into private working storage immediately. Proposed service targets, prices and internal schedules need owner adoption and capacity verification.
- All legal templates are DRAFT ATTORNEY REVIEW. Fill required fields, resolve the separate decisions register, obtain qualified counsel review, then approve a version before any client use or signature.
- Pricing figures are illustrative planning inputs, not quotes or market-rate claims. Tax, payroll and insurance applicability requires review by the appropriate professional.
- Documents do not authorize deployment, DNS changes, account creation, purchases, payments, automated messages or signatures. Research and preparation can proceed; execution requires the owner's applicable approval.

## Public repository boundary

This repository is public. Store only blank templates, explicitly fictional examples and public references here. Completed client records, proposals, contracts, incident evidence, employee records, bank details, tax forms, live costs and private contacts belong in restricted company storage. Never commit passwords, keys, tokens, card data, government identifiers or credential-recovery material. A private-storage link can itself expose an identifier; keep completed record indexes outside Git too.

For each real engagement create a private folder using a neutral client/project ID, copy only the needed templates, restrict access, and record the location in the private client register. Keep legal execution copies immutable and preserve the approved version. The repo is the template source, not a CRM, payroll database or secret vault.

## Folder map

- `clients/`, `sales/`, `projects/`: intake, agreements handoff, delivery and approval gates
- `finance/`, `services/`: cost-first quoting, collections, close and draft tiers
- `hosting/`, `support/`, `security/`: service inventory, recovery and access controls
- `legal/`: review drafts, shared variables, review and agreement-selection guidance
- `employees/`, `vendors/`, `compliance/`: people, supplier and jurisdiction checklists
- `metrics/`, `tooling/`, `automations/`: measurable operations and later improvements
- `validation/`: reproducible checks and preservation evidence; no real operating data

## Working rules

The owner is initially accountable for every area. A named delivery/support/bookkeeping assignee may do work, but the owner retains pricing, scope, vendor spend, legal, production and people decisions. An unassigned duty stays with the owner, never an imaginary department.

Each checklist instance records owner, due date, evidence and exceptions. An unchecked gate blocks the next stage unless the owner records a lawful, bounded exception; legal obligations and client approvals cannot be waived by an internal exception. Review templates quarterly and after a material incident or legal change. Use ISO dates with an explicit timezone for deadlines and incidents.
