# Draft service catalog

Version 0.1 · Prepared 2026-10-13 · Proposed operating practice pending owner adoption

All amounts below are illustrative USD planning ranges, not approved quotes, market benchmarks or current supplier prices. They show a cost-first starting model. Assumed fully loaded target contribution margin is 45%, combined variable fees 3%, no fixed transaction fee: price = cost ÷ 0.52, rounded upward to whole dollars. The model workbook is authoritative for real fee/tax/commission inputs. No service exists until the owner adopts its scope and a reviewed agreement is executed.

## Basic Website

- For: A small informational site with supplied content.
- Included: Defined page count, responsive build, standard contact form, agreed accessibility testing and launch handoff.
- Excluded: E-commerce, custom backend, branding/content production, ongoing edits.
- Internal setup cost scenario: $500–$1,800; monthly non-percentage cost scenario: $20–$70.
- Derived setup price scenario: $962–$3,462; monthly price scenario: $39–$135. Taxes and client-paid vendors excluded; real quote uses actual costs and fee structure.
- Typical planning support burden: 0.25–1 hour/month if support purchased; validate with time logs before selling capacity.
- Common add-ons: Extra pages, copy, migration.
- Custom quote/review trigger: Login, regulated data, custom integrations or complex migration.

## Managed Website

- For: A business needing hosting plus bounded routine care.
- Included: Agreed hosting, backups, monitoring, updates and a defined content/support allowance.
- Excluded: Unlimited edits, 24/7 human coverage, redesign, unsupported plugins.
- Internal setup cost scenario: $750–$2,500; monthly non-percentage cost scenario: $100–$250.
- Derived setup price scenario: $1,443–$4,808; monthly price scenario: $193–$481. Taxes and client-paid vendors excluded; real quote uses actual costs and fee structure.
- Typical planning support burden: 1–3 hours/month; validate with time logs before selling capacity.
- Common add-ons: Additional content allowance, reporting.
- Custom quote/review trigger: High traffic, urgent response promise, plugins with no support.

## Managed Hosting

- For: A supported application/site with known requirements.
- Included: Documented environment, TLS, monitoring, backups and defined maintenance.
- Excluded: Application feature work, client devices/email unless listed, blanket SLA.
- Internal setup cost scenario: $250–$1,000; monthly non-percentage cost scenario: $60–$220.
- Derived setup price scenario: $481–$1,924; monthly price scenario: $116–$424. Taxes and client-paid vendors excluded; real quote uses actual costs and fee structure.
- Typical planning support burden: 0.5–2 hours/month; validate with time logs before selling capacity.
- Common add-ons: Storage, restore tests, extra environments.
- Custom quote/review trigger: Unsupported stack, special region, high availability or recovery need.

## Custom Website or Application

- For: A scoped business workflow beyond a basic site.
- Included: Discovery, agreed architecture/deliverables, tests, docs, acceptance and launch.
- Excluded: Unbounded discovery, unlimited changes, third-party availability guarantees.
- Internal setup cost scenario: $3,000–$15,000; monthly non-percentage cost scenario: $150–$800.
- Derived setup price scenario: $5,770–$28,847; monthly price scenario: $289–$1,539. Taxes and client-paid vendors excluded; real quote uses actual costs and fee structure.

- Common add-ons: Discovery sprint, migration, training.
- Custom quote/review trigger: Always custom quote after discovery; ranges are scenario bounds only.

- Internal setup cost scenario: $1,000–$6,000; monthly non-percentage cost scenario: $75–$350.
- Derived setup price scenario: $1,924–$11,539; monthly price scenario: $145–$674. Taxes and client-paid vendors excluded; real quote uses actual costs and fee structure.
- Typical planning support burden: 1–4 hours/month; validate with time logs before selling capacity.
- Common add-ons: Additional connector, reconciliation report.
- Custom quote/review trigger: Financial/regulated actions, bidirectional sync, undocumented APIs.

## Payment Integration

- For: A client-owned merchant account with hosted/tokenized flow.
- Included: Agreed sandbox implementation, verified webhooks, idempotency, reconciliation and security handoff.
- Excluded: Processing fees, merchant approval, tax/surcharge advice, custody or card storage.
- Internal setup cost scenario: $1,800–$8,000; monthly non-percentage cost scenario: $120–$500.
- Derived setup price scenario: $3,462–$15,385; monthly price scenario: $231–$962. Taxes and client-paid vendors excluded; real quote uses actual costs and fee structure.
- Typical planning support burden: 1–5 hours/month; validate with time logs before selling capacity.
- Common add-ons: Additional payment method, reporting.
- Custom quote/review trigger: Always custom quote and payment/privacy review; no blanket PCI certification.

## Custom Software Maintenance

- For: An accepted codebase with documented ownership and supportability.
- Included: Agreed dependency/security updates, defect triage, maintenance window and hours.
- Excluded: New features, inherited defects beyond baseline, perpetual compatibility.
- Internal setup cost scenario: $500–$2,500; monthly non-percentage cost scenario: $150–$1,000.
- Derived setup price scenario: $962–$4,808; monthly price scenario: $289–$1,924. Taxes and client-paid vendors excluded; real quote uses actual costs and fee structure.
- Typical planning support burden: 2–10 hours/month; validate with time logs before selling capacity.
- Common add-ons: Extra support block, upgrade project.
- Custom quote/review trigger: Unknown code quality, missing source/tests, end-of-life components.

## Scope controls

Specify pages/users/traffic/storage, environments, supported integrations, support hours, maintenance tasks, response window and recovery objectives in the SOW. Unused support hours, rollover, after-hours work and overages require explicit terms. A recurring fee does not imply unlimited development or guaranteed uptime. Reprice only under the agreement with the required notice and consent.
