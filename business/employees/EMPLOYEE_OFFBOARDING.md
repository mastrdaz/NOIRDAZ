# Employee offboarding checklist

Owner: [[HR_OWNER]]. Technical owner: [[ACCESS_OWNER]]. Employee reference: [[EMPLOYEE_ID]]. Separation effective: [[SEPARATION_DATE_TIME_AND_ZONE]]. Completed record belongs in [[PRIVATE_EMPLOYEE_RECORD_LOCATION]]. Draft workflow; counsel reviews the actual separation and work-state requirements.

## Before notification

- [ ] Owner/counsel approves the decision, communication, documentation and any agreement; check protected leave, accommodation, discrimination, retaliation, benefit and contractual issues.
- [ ] Record actual work states. Final-pay deadlines and commission treatment can differ outside Wisconsin.
- [ ] Inventory client work, incidents, recurring jobs, delegated approvals, repository/cloud ownership, equipment and access.
- [ ] Identify preservation/legal holds before changing accounts or deleting anything; transfer business ownership without reading unrelated personal material.
- [ ] Coordinate notification and access revocation time, including an immediate plan when risk warrants it.

## Pay and benefits

- [ ] Reconcile all hours, overtime, earned wages, commissions/bonuses, approved expenses and PTO according to reviewed law/policy/agreement.
- [ ] Wisconsin’s general rule is payment on the regular pay schedule after separation. Counsel checks special circumstances and other-state rules. Do not hold wages pending equipment return. [DWD wage payment](https://dwd.wisconsin.gov/er/laborstandards/wages.htm)
- [ ] No blanket damage/equipment deduction or forfeiture of earned commissions. Counsel reviews any proposed deduction and the facts before payroll acts.
- [ ] Provide a private calculation and a contact for later commission adjustments or disputes; avoid retroactive pay-plan changes.
- [ ] Payroll/benefits adviser determines notices, continuation/conversion rights and final benefit dates; do not assume small headcount removes every requirement.
- [ ] Obtain financial approval for the actual payroll/payment, then retain settlement evidence.

## At separation

- [ ] Use [Equipment and access checklist](EQUIPMENT_ACCESS_CHECKLIST.md) and the security offboarding process.
- [ ] Revoke identity-provider sessions, email access, password-vault membership, GitHub, cloud, hosting, DNS/registrar, CRM, support, finance and client access.
- [ ] Disable or rotate tokens, SSH keys, app passwords, shared secrets and recovery methods the person could access, coordinating service continuity.
- [ ] Transfer service-account ownership, scheduled jobs, billing contacts and incident on-call assignments; verify the owner retains access.
- [ ] Recover equipment and verify return condition; do not erase held evidence or the employee’s personal device without authority.
- [ ] Reassign open tickets/projects and give approved continuity notices only to appropriate clients.

## Close

- [ ] Verify access actually fails and services still run; log evidence and exceptions.
- [ ] Confirm final pay, benefits notices and later commission review ownership.
- [ ] Preserve employment records under the approved schedule and any hold. I-9 destruction eligibility is the later of hire + three years or termination + one year; keep it throughout employment. [USCIS](https://www.uscis.gov/sites/default/files/document/guides/E3en.pdf)
- [ ] Record [[OFFBOARDING_COMPLETE_DATE]], [[OPEN_ITEMS_OWNER]], [[FINAL_PAY_CONFIRMATION_REFERENCE]] and [[ACCESS_REVOCATION_REFERENCE]].

General sources checked 2026-10-13. This checklist does not authorize account deletion, waive wages or establish a retention exception.
