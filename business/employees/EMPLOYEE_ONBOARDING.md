# Employee onboarding checklist

Use one private copy per employee. Owner: [[HR_OWNER]]. Start: [[START_DATE]]. Employee reference: [[EMPLOYEE_ID]]. Status: draft workflow for an owner and up to five employees. Do not put names, SSNs, bank details, ID images, immigration documents, medical information or signed personnel agreements in this repository. Completed record: [[PRIVATE_EMPLOYEE_RECORD_LOCATION]].

## Before making the offer

- [ ] Owner approves business need, duties, reporting line, budget and actual work location using [Role template](ROLE_TEMPLATE.md).
- [ ] CPA/counsel reviews employee/contractor status and state/federal overtime classification based on actual duties; record [[CLASSIFICATION_REVIEW_REFERENCE]].
- [ ] Confirm compensation, pay frequency, overtime process, commission terms, benefits/PTO and any expense reimbursement rules.
- [ ] Counsel reviews offer and confidentiality/IP terms; record approved document versions. Do not promise unapproved benefits, job duration or restrictive covenants.
- [ ] Complete [Multistate review](../compliance/MULTISTATE_TRIGGER_CHECKLIST.md) if any work is outside Wisconsin.

## After acceptance and before the first day

- [ ] Signed offer and applicable reviewed agreements stored privately.
- [ ] Complete [Payroll setup](PAYROLL_SETUP_CHECKLIST.md), including insurance, UI/withholding and pay-date readiness.
- [ ] Designate [[I9_REVIEWER]] and a lawful document-review method. Remote hiring does not automatically permit video-only I-9 review.
- [ ] Provide current Form I-9 instructions and acceptable-document list; employee chooses acceptable documents. Do not request a particular document based on perceived nationality or citizenship.
- [ ] Obtain required tax forms through the payroll provider’s secure process; do not collect them in email or GitHub.
- [ ] Complete [Equipment and access](EQUIPMENT_ACCESS_CHECKLIST.md); schedule orientation and [[FIRST_WEEK_CONTACT]].

## Timed hiring obligations

General federal/Wisconsin timing checked 2026-10-13; verify current form editions and exceptions with the responsible reviewer.

- [ ] Employee completes I-9 Section 1 after accepting the offer and no later than the first day of employment.
- [ ] Employer completes Section 2 within three business days after that first day. For employment lasting fewer than three business days, complete by the first day. [USCIS instructions](https://www.uscis.gov/sites/default/files/document/forms/i-9instr.pdf)
- [ ] Wisconsin new hire submitted within 20 days of starting work; this also covers specified returns after an unpaid interval exceeding 60 days. Save confirmation privately and verify whether payroll provider performed it. [DWD new hires](https://dwd.wisconsin.gov/uinh/)
- [ ] Calendar any required reverification using current USCIS guidance; do not reverify indiscriminately.

## First day and first month

- [ ] Explain duties, approval limits, timekeeping, pay questions, expenses, reporting concerns and anti-retaliation expectations.
- [ ] Give applicable workplace notices and explain how remote workers can access them. [DWD posters](https://www.dwd.wisconsin.gov/dwd/workplace-posters/)
- [ ] Complete [Training checklist](TRAINING_CHECKLIST.md); keep client/production access limited until training and authorization are complete.
- [ ] Review first pay stub with employee for operational errors without exposing it to other staff.
- [ ] At proposed day-7 and day-30 check-ins, confirm responsibilities, workload, access and support needs; record actions.

Exit gate: [[HR_OWNER]] verifies statutory submissions, access inventory, training, payroll accuracy and unresolved issues. An unchecked legal/payroll gate is escalated immediately; it is not waived by a manager’s checklist signature.
