# Sales compensation plan template

Attorney and payroll review draft. Do not issue, sign or use to calculate live pay until approved. This template selects no wage rate, commission percentage, exemption or contractor status. Use a separate reviewed agreement for a genuine independent sales contractor.

## Calculation worksheet

Proposed calculation: eligible commission base × approved rate × approved credit share, plus approved incentive amounts. Separately calculate any payroll-required minimum-wage make-up, overtime adjustment or other wages. A negative adjustment must not flow automatically into payroll.

## Controls before adoption

- [ ] Counsel reviews state/federal law, earned-wage definitions, deductions, separation treatment and prospectively effective changes.
- [ ] Payroll confirms timekeeping and regular-rate/overtime handling. Commission pay, a sales title or salary alone does not establish an exemption.
- [ ] Draft policy choice: use an hourly nonexempt base plus clearly documented incentive terms unless counsel approves another arrangement. This is a proposal, not the employee’s classification.
- [ ] Define reasonable statement review and correction process; internal review windows do not waive legal rights.
- [ ] Owner approves affordability and cash timing; employee receives the approved plan before the applicable performance period.
- [ ] Retain signed version privately and apply changes prospectively following legal review; never silently change earned amounts.
