# Employee training checklist

Draft operating standard. Employee reference: [[EMPLOYEE_ID]]. Trainer: [[TRAINER]]. Completed evidence: [[PRIVATE_TRAINING_RECORD_LOCATION]]. Role-specific access opens after the relevant demonstration and approval.

| Timing proposed | Topic | Demonstration or evidence |
|---|---|---|
| First day | Role, approvals, timekeeping, payroll questions and expense process | Employee records sample time and finds escalation contact |
| First day | Respectful workplace, reporting concerns, non-retaliation, accommodations and safety | Employee knows confidential reporting route and emergency contacts |
| Before system access | Password manager, MFA, least privilege, phishing and device security | Demonstrate secure sign-in and report a simulated suspicious request |
| Before client data | Data classification, client permissions, retention and approved storage | Classify a fictional example and choose its storage location |
| Before production work | Change approval, test/staging separation, rollback, backups and incident response | Complete a sandbox change and explain rollback/escalation |
| Before support duty | Ticket intake, severity, response commitments and escalation | Triage a sample outage; do not promise an unapproved SLA |
| Before sales authority | Scope/pricing approvals, contract handoff and commission records | Build a fictional quote and identify required approvals |
| Within first month | Client lifecycle, handoffs, acceptance and offboarding | Walk through a sample client without using real private data |
| Quarterly proposal | Phishing/access/incident refresher and role gaps | Short exercise plus corrective actions |
| On policy or role change | Only changed responsibilities and procedures | Record revised version and successful demonstration |

- [ ] Trainer logs [[TRAINING_TOPIC]], [[POLICY_VERSION]], [[COMPLETION_DATE]], [[DEMONSTRATION_RESULT]] and [[FOLLOW_UP_ACTION]].
- [ ] Required training time is recorded and sent to payroll; classification/pay rules are reviewed rather than assuming training is unpaid.
- [ ] Legal or customer-required certifications/training are verified separately; do not invent certifications or imply completion from a checklist.
- [ ] Employee can explain whom to contact when unsure; unresolved gaps have an owner and due date.
