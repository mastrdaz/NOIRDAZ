# Payroll setup checklist

Owner: [[PAYROLL_OWNER]]. Reviewer: [[CPA_CONTACT]]. First pay date: [[FIRST_PAY_DATE]]. Draft preparation checklist; actual submissions, agreements and money movements need their applicable approvals. Keep completed records and all identifiers in [[PRIVATE_PAYROLL_RECORD_LOCATION]].

## Employer readiness

- [ ] Confirm legal employer, entity tax treatment and EIN with CPA. Determine whether and how the owner belongs on payroll; do not assume owner draws are wages or vice versa.
- [ ] Complete applicable federal/state/local registrations and verify notices, account status, assigned tax rates and deposit frequencies.
- [ ] Check Wisconsin UI and workers’ compensation triggers in [Wisconsin requirements](../compliance/WISCONSIN_REQUIREMENTS.md). Insurer confirms effective coverage, worker categories and work locations.
- [ ] Select payroll provider after reviewing tax filing responsibility, supported states, funding lead time, additional fees, errors, cancellation and data export.
- [ ] Assign a person to employer registrations, new-hire reports, deposits, quarterly returns, W-2s, corrections and agency notices; provider marketing is not a responsibility matrix.
- [ ] Have CPA confirm payroll tax liabilities and cash needed separately from wage expense and software fees.

## Employee readiness

- [ ] Confirm accepted offer, approved classification, actual work location, start date, rate, pay frequency and recorded hours.
- [ ] Securely collect federal W-4, applicable Wisconsin WT-4 or other state certificates, and authorized payment instructions. Track I-9 separately.
- [ ] Review commissions, bonuses, benefits, reimbursements, deductions, garnishments and leave treatment with payroll/counsel.
- [ ] Establish [[WORKWEEK_START]] and timekeeping for all compensable work, including required training and support work after hours.
- [ ] Confirm wage statements show required information; establish an accessible correction route.

## Test and authorize

- [ ] Provider/CPA reviews first-pay calculation, regular rate/overtime, taxes, benefit deductions and employer liabilities using approved data.
- [ ] Confirm employee payment details through a secure process and verify changed details out of band.
- [ ] Confirm funds and bank lead time; no test debit or live run without required financial authorization.
- [ ] Owner reviews counterparty, total payroll/taxes/fees, recurrence, terms and risk, approves in principle, then separately gives explicit final confirmation immediately before the authorized execution. Reapprove material changes.
- [ ] Save approval, payroll register and submission confirmation privately; reconcile settlement and agency acknowledgements afterward.
- [ ] Add filings/deposits to [Annual calendar](../compliance/ANNUAL_COMPLIANCE_CALENDAR.md); investigate failed debit/rejected filing promptly.

Sources checked 2026-10-13: [IRS employer tax guide](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p15), [Wisconsin withholding](https://www.revenue.wi.gov/Pages/FAQS/pcs-with.aspx), [DWD wage payment](https://dwd.wisconsin.gov/er/laborstandards/wages.htm). Exact rates, withholding and deposit schedules remain CPA/provider inputs.
