# Annual compliance and operating calendar

Owner: [[COMPLIANCE_CALENDAR_OWNER]]. Status: proposed control, awaiting owner/CPA confirmation. Sources checked 2026-10-13. Use America/Chicago for Wisconsin agency scheduling and confirm each portal’s submission cutoff. Calendar reminders are not filings or payments.

## Set up once

- [ ] Confirm entity, fiscal year, registrations, payroll start, tax elections, state footprint and assigned filing frequencies.
- [ ] Enter real due dates in [[PRIVATE_CALENDAR_LOCATION]], with owner, reviewer, preparation date and receipt location.
- [ ] Set internal reminders 30 and 7 days before annual deadlines, and preparation deadlines before each payroll/tax submission. These are proposed operating choices.
- [ ] Recheck statutory weekend/holiday adjustments, disaster relief and agency notices each year. Never shift a deadline automatically without checking the applicable rule.
