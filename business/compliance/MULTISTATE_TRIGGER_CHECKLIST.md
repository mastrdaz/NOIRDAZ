# Multistate trigger checklist

Status: screening process for owner, CPA, counsel and insurer; not a 50-state legal conclusion. Checked 2026-10-13. Owner: [[COMPLIANCE_OWNER]]. Complete before activity in a new jurisdiction and review monthly sales/work-location changes.

## Open a review when any answer is yes

- [ ] Employee, owner or contractor will work from another state, even temporarily or remotely.
- [ ] A worker changes residence, home office, travel pattern or customer worksite.
- [ ] Noirdaz opens an office, stores equipment, uses dedicated infrastructure, hires an in-state representative, or buys an existing operation there.
- [ ] A customer uses software/services in another state, including users in several states under one contract.
- [ ] Revenue or transaction activity approaches a state’s current economic-nexus threshold, or a marketplace begins collecting tax.
- [ ] A project adds regulated data, consumer subscriptions, public-sector customers, payment handling, or industry-specific contractual duties.

These are review triggers, not assertions that each creates registration or tax liability. Separate entity qualification, sales/use tax, income/franchise/gross-receipts tax, payroll, employment law and insurance. Clearing one does not clear the others. [SBA registration overview](https://www.sba.gov/counseling/launch-your-business/).

## Threshold and payroll safeguards

- Maintain a state-by-state sales ledger: gross sales, taxable sales, exempt sales, marketplace sales and transaction counts, with the relevant measurement period. Each state chooses its own test and what counts.
- Wisconsin’s remote-seller exception currently uses gross sales exceeding $100,000 in the previous or current calendar year; the 200-transaction test was removed. This is a remote-seller rule, not a home-state exemption for a Wisconsin business. Do not copy it to other states. [Wisconsin DOR](https://www.revenue.wi.gov/Pages/FAQS/ise-remote-sellers.aspx)
- Wisconsin reciprocity with Illinois, Indiana, Kentucky and Michigan concerns qualifying wage withholding with required documentation. It does not settle UI, workers’ compensation, entity registration or other state taxes. [DOR withholding](https://www.revenue.wi.gov/Pages/FAQS/pcs-with.aspx)
- No worker starts the new-location arrangement until payroll/provider and insurer readiness are documented. Decide a workable location-change notice process before hiring.
- A payroll provider’s multi-state feature does not perform every employer registration or employment-law review automatically. Assign those tasks by name.
