# Hosting transfer and shutdown

Version 0.1 · Prepared 2026-10-13 · Proposed operating practice pending owner adoption

## Plan

Verify client authority and executed exit terms. Inventory website/source, uploaded files, databases, domains/DNS, certificates, email, analytics, scheduled jobs, webhooks, licenses, logs and backups. Identify what is client-owned, transferable, third-party licensed or shared infrastructure. Agree formats, secure recipient, timeline, transition assistance and approved fees.

## Transfer without avoidable loss

- [ ] Produce export with date/version, checksums and restoration instructions
- [ ] Validate database row/count or business totals and representative files
- [ ] Transfer through approved encrypted route to a verified recipient
- [ ] Confirm receipt and test restore at the destination where agreed
- [ ] Coordinate domain/DNS changes separately; preserve mail and active integrations
- [ ] Reassign client-owned accounts using provider-supported invitations, not emailed passwords
- [ ] Rotate shared credentials; deliver individual access through secure handoff
- [ ] Verify new service before ending old service when the agreed plan allows overlap

## Close

Record actual responsibility end and unresolved issues; revoke Noirdaz access after transfer; cancel only authorized vendor/billing services; reconcile final charges and prepaid credits. Apply approved retention/legal holds and backup expiry. Provide a truthful deletion confirmation describing active-system deletion, retained categories and backup aging. Keep essential contract/accounting evidence in restricted storage. Do not delete client data solely because a bill is disputed.
