# Owner decisions before adoption

Version 0.1 · Prepared 2026-10-13 · Proposed operating practice pending owner adoption

This register keeps business choices separate from the templates. Nothing below is approved merely because it appears in the repository. Record actual decisions privately with approver, date, effective version and evidence.

| Decision | Information needed | Reviewer and gate |
|---|---|---|
| Contracting identity and authority | Exact legal name/entity/DBA, formation records, addresses, signers | Owner and counsel before any agreement |
| Standard commercial model | Deposit, billing due days, recurring start, cancellation/renewal, refund and late-charge choices | Owner, CPA and counsel before quote |
| Services and capacity | Adopted tiers, supported stacks, limits, hours and client count | Owner before selling |
| Pricing | Actual loaded labor, supplier quotes, fee basis, target/floor margin and pilot limits | Owner before each quote |
| Support/recovery | Staffed coverage, response/update targets, backup/retention and tested RPO/RTO | Owner and technical reviewer before promise |
| Legal risk allocation | Liability caps/carveouts, indemnity, IP, warranty, law/venue, term, notices | Counsel, owner and insurer before use |
| Data boundaries | Allowed/declined regulated workloads, regions, subprocessors and processing terms | Owner/counsel/security before access |
| Insurance | Actual policies, limits, exclusions, endorsements and notice rules | Broker and counsel before promises/hiring |
| Finance and tax | Entity treatment, accounts, accounting method, SKU taxability, reserves and filing calendar | CPA/bookkeeper before activation |
| Personnel | Actual role, wage/classification, work state, commission terms, payroll and coverage | Owner, counsel, payroll and broker before hire |
| Tooling | Required seats/features, private storage, export, total/recurring cost and terms | Owner before any account/purchase/access |
| Approval authority | Named owner/backup, who may prepare and who may authorize each action | Owner before delegation |
| Template adoption | Approved versions, review cadence, execution-copy retention | Owner after professional review |

Legal detail: [owner and counsel decision register](legal/review/OWNER_DECISIONS_REGISTER.md). Each unresolved legal placeholder is a signing gate. An illustrative price or proposed support cadence is not an owner decision.
