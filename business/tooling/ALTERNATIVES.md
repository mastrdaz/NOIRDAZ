# Software alternatives and selection notes

Research checked 2026-10-13. Official vendor pages are linked for revalidation. Prices are USD before tax; annual equivalents may require full-year payment. Promotional discounts, trials and AI credits are not permanent free plans. These are selection options, not purchase instructions.

| Category | Alternative to recommended choice | Verified starting point | Choose it when; main tradeoff |
|---|---|---|---|
| CRM | Zoho CRM Free instead of HubSpot | Three users; pricing page describes export limits, so test backup cadence; [pricing](https://www.zoho.com/crm/zohocrm-pricing.html) | Third sales user matters or Zoho consolidation is useful. Test actual export limits, fields and integration tier; avoid buying a broad suite just for pipeline stages. |
| Invoicing | Zoho Invoice instead of Wave invoices | Free; two users, three projects, 500 invoices/year, two organizations; [pricing](https://www.zoho.com/invoice/pricing/) | Dedicated invoicing/timesheets suit the workflow. It is not a substitute for the accounting ledger; avoid double entry. Inactivity over 180 days can lead to deletion under its policy. |
| Accounting | Zoho Books instead of Wave | U.S. Free: annual revenue no more than $50,000, one user plus accountant, 1,000 invoices/year. Standard $20/month or $15/month annual billing; [pricing](https://www.zoho.com/us/books/pricing/) | Structured bookkeeping and accountant fit favor it. Confirm bank feed/reporting needs and eligibility; no assumption that Noirdaz qualifies for the free tier. |
| Payroll | Patriot Full Service instead of Gusto | Regular price $37/month + $5/worker paid; additional state filing $12/month per extra state; no permanent free payroll tier; [pricing](https://www.patriotsoftware.com/pricing/) | Lower single-state software cost is important. Compare support, funding lead times and integrations; Basic is not equivalent to Full Service tax filing. |
| Ticketing | Freshdesk Growth instead of Zoho Desk Free | $19/agent/month billed annually; current page offers trials and paid plans, not a verified permanent free tier; [pricing](https://www.freshworks.com/freshdesk/pricing/) | Paid helpdesk features justify per-agent costs. AI sessions, connectors and other usage can add charges; disable until reviewed. |
| Password management | 1Password Teams Starter instead of Bitwarden Teams | FAQ quotes $24.95/month billed annually, $299.40/year for ten included members; [pricing](https://1password.com/pricing/business) | Team prefers its workflow or wants included seat headroom. Minimum package means one-to-six people do not pay simply $2.49 each. Confirm checkout total because displayed per-user headline is rounded. |
| Documentation | Notion instead of Markdown plus existing private document store | Free: individual unlimited blocks, limited blocks for 2+ members, 5 MB/file, seven-day history, ten guests; [pricing](https://www.notion.com/pricing) | Nontechnical editing and structured pages matter. Team use may need paid seats; verify U.S. checkout price and export all critical content. Never publish a personnel/client page by accident. |
| Contracts and e-signature | Dropbox Sign Essentials instead of Zoho Sign Free | $15/month annual equivalent, $180 billed yearly; [pricing](https://sign.dropbox.com/products/dropbox-sign/pricing) | Owner sends enough contracts to justify a paid sender plan. Review signer authentication, limits, integrations and audit exports; exclude temporary promotions. |
| Uptime monitoring | Uptime Kuma self-hosted instead of Better Stack | Open-source self-hosted project; no SaaS subscription price claimed; [project](https://github.com/louislam/uptime-kuma) | Owner can maintain an independent monitor host. Hosting, updates, backups and alert delivery have real cost; never monitor only from the same host whose outage is being tested. |
| Backup monitoring | Healthchecks.io instead of Better Stack heartbeats | Free 20 jobs/100 log entries per job; Business $20/month for 100 jobs or $192/year; [pricing](https://healthchecks.io/pricing/), [account-limit rules](https://healthchecks.io/docs/faq/) | Scheduled jobs need a simple dedicated checker. Agency accounts cannot multiply free capacity by creating extra accounts; client-owned accounts have specific ownership rules. |
| Project management | Trello instead of GitHub Projects | Free: ten collaborators and ten boards/workspace, 10 MB/file, 250 command runs/month; Standard $5/user/month annually or $6 monthly; [pricing](https://trello.com/pricing) | Nontechnical team wants straightforward boards. Guest billing, export format and paid automation need review. Do not maintain duplicate authoritative task lists. |
| Business analytics | Metabase instead of Calc | Open Source free license/self-hosting; cloud Starter $100/month or $1,080/year, first five users included, additional-user fees; [pricing](https://www.metabase.com/pricing) | Repeated database reporting saves more time than maintaining the platform costs. Hosting, patching, database access and permission design remain work; defer initially. |

## Decision scorecard

For each shortlisted tool, owner records [[BUSINESS_NEED]], [[CURRENT_TOOL_GAP]], [[SEATS_AND_USAGE]], [[TOTAL_ANNUAL_COST]], [[EXPORT_TEST_RESULT]], [[SECURITY_REVIEW]], [[MIGRATION_COST]], [[CANCELLATION_TERMS]] and [[OWNER_DECISION]]. Weight practical fit and data portability ahead of feature counts. If the existing tool meets the requirement, no replacement is necessary.

Do not run trials against real payroll, credentials or client records as a research shortcut. Free software can still create data-sharing obligations, contract terms and future costs. Research and comparison do not authorize setup, payments or subscriptions.
