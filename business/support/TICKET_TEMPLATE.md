# Support ticket template

Version 0.1 · Prepared 2026-10-13 · Proposed operating practice pending owner adoption

Private ticket [[TICKET_ID]] · Client/service [[CLIENT_ID]] / [[SERVICE_ID]]

- Reported at/timezone/channel/by: [[REPORTED_AT]] / [[TIMEZONE]] / [[CHANNEL]] / [[REPORTER]]
- Authorized contact and entitlement checked: [[SUPPORT_AUTHORIZATION]]
- Summary/type/priority and reason: [[ISSUE_SUMMARY]] / [[TICKET_TYPE]] / [[PRIORITY]] / [[PRIORITY_REASON]]
- Business impact, affected users, data/security concern: [[IMPACT]]
- Expected versus actual, reproduction, recent change: [[DIAGNOSTIC_CONTEXT]]
- Redacted evidence/private log reference: [[EVIDENCE_REFERENCE]]
- Owner, backup, next update due: [[TICKET_OWNER]] / [[BACKUP_OWNER]] / [[NEXT_UPDATE_TIME]]
- Actions with times/results/approvals: [[ACTION_LOG]]
- Billable change estimate and approval if applicable: [[BILLABLE_APPROVAL]]
- Resolution/workaround, verification and client confirmation: [[RESOLUTION_EVIDENCE]]
- Time/cost, linked incident/change/problem: [[WORK_LOG]]
- Closed/reopened reason/date and follow-up: [[CLOSURE_RECORD]]

No secrets, authentication tokens, full personal-data exports, PAN or CVV in tickets. Move sensitive evidence to the restricted incident location with minimal access.
