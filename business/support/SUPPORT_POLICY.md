# Internal support procedure

Version 0.1 · Prepared 2026-10-13 · Proposed operating practice pending owner adoption

The executed support exhibit defines entitlement, contact method, coverage window, response targets and charges. This internal procedure does not create an SLA. Proposed staffing is owner-led business-hours support; do not advertise 24/7 response without verified staffing and approved terms.

1. Log every request with client/service, reporter, authorization, symptoms, impact, priority, time and channel.
2. Check entitlement and distinguish incident/defect/how-to/enhancement. Do not deny urgent security triage merely because commercial classification is uncertain; contain within authorized scope and escalate the decision.
3. Confirm reproducibility, affected users/data and recent changes; ask for minimal redacted evidence, never passwords or full card data.
4. Acknowledge, assign one owner and give next update time. A response target is not a resolution guarantee.
5. Diagnose safely; document work and time. Obtain approval before extra billable work, production changes or purchases.
6. Verify the outcome with tests/client, document workaround and remaining risk, and close with evidence.
7. Review repeated requests for a root-cause fix or scoped improvement rather than hiding them in unlimited support.

Use [priority levels](PRIORITY_LEVELS.md), [ticket template](TICKET_TEMPLATE.md), [escalation](ESCALATION_PROCESS.md) and [outage drafts](OUTAGE_COMMUNICATION_TEMPLATE.md). Client-facing contractual language is in `../legal/templates/SUPPORT_POLICY_EXHIBIT.md`; reconcile it before adoption.
