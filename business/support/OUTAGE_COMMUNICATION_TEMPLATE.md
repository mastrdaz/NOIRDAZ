# Outage communication drafts

Version 0.1 · Prepared 2026-10-13 · Proposed operating practice pending owner adoption

Drafts for owner approval to the verified affected client contacts. Do not send unrelated client details or unverified breach conclusions. Required statutory/contractual notices need counsel-guided handling and may need a different form.

Subject: [[SERVICE_NAME]] service issue

## Update

We have confirmed [[VERIFIED_FACTS]]. We have completed [[ACTIONS_COMPLETED]] and are working on [[NEXT_STEP]]. [[RESTORATION_ESTIMATE_WITH_BASIS_OR_NO_ESTIMATE]]. The next update is due by [[NEXT_UPDATE_TIME]].

## Recovery

Service was restored at [[RECOVERY_TIME_WITH_TIMEZONE]]. We verified [[RECOVERY_TESTS]]. [[REMAINING_LIMITATIONS]]. Please report any continuing issue through [[SUPPORT_CHANNEL]]. We will provide [[AGREED_FOLLOW_UP]] by [[FOLLOW_UP_DATE]].

## Review summary

Impact and duration [[INCIDENT_IMPACT]]. Confirmed cause or investigation status [[CAUSE_STATUS]]. Recovery [[RECOVERY_ACTIONS]]. Corrective actions and owners [[CORRECTIVE_ACTIONS]]. Avoid “no data affected” unless evidence supports that conclusion; state what remains under investigation.
