# Expense categories

Version 0.1 · Prepared 2026-10-13 · Proposed operating practice pending owner adoption

Use these operating labels; the bookkeeper/CPA owns the chart of accounts, deductibility, capitalization and tax reporting.

| Category | Typical records | Allocation/control |
|---|---|---|
| Hosting, storage, bandwidth and database | Provider invoices, usage | Direct service COGS when attributable; reconcile shared allocation |
| Domains and email | Registrar/license renewal | Identify client-paid versus company-owned |
| Software/licenses | Subscription, seats, contract | Separate client delivery from company administration |
| Legal | Engagement, invoice | Separate formation, contracts and dispute work for CPA |
| Insurance | Policy, endorsements, invoices | Coverage periods and prepaid treatment |
| Payroll | Payroll register, taxes, benefits | Restricted access; wage and employer cost separated |
| Contractor expense | Agreement, invoice, classification/W-9 evidence | Tax forms private; verify reporting rules annually |
| Sales and marketing | Commission, campaign receipts | Written commission plan; no promised spend from estimate |
| Hardware | Invoice, asset and custodian | CPA determines capitalization/depreciation |
| Travel | Business purpose, receipt, mileage | Approved policy and current tax treatment |
| Training | Course, attendee, relevance | Approval and receipt |
| Banking and processing | Bank/processor statements | Gross-to-net reconciliation |
| Taxes | Return/payment confirmation | Separate payroll, sales tax liability and entity/owner taxes |
| Office and administration | Rent, supplies, communication | Document business use |

Do not expense collected sales tax as ordinary revenue/cost. Owner draws, loans and capital contributions are not sales. Record client reimbursables separately to avoid hiding pass-through margin and principal/agent issues. Every entry needs date, vendor, purpose, amount, currency, approval and receipt reference.
