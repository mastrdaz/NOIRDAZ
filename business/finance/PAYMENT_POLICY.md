# Payment and collections procedure

Version 0.1 · Prepared 2026-10-13 · Proposed operating practice pending owner adoption

Follow the executed agreement for due dates, disputes, late charges, refund rights, suspension and termination. Proposed reminder timing below is an internal workflow and does not create extra fees or rights.

## Collections

- At due date: reconcile bank/processor before marking overdue; check for duplicate/misdirected invoice or dispute.
- 3 business days past due: prepare a factual reminder with invoice, balance, due date and payment link for owner approval.
- 10 business days past due: owner contacts billing decision-maker through approved channel and records dispute/payment plan.
- Before any suspension: verify contractual authority, required notice/cure, data export and business/safety consequences; obtain owner decision. Never disable domain ownership or retained unrelated services.
- For a substantive dispute: segregate disputed and undisputed amounts, collect evidence, pause unsupported escalation and seek counsel if needed.

Late interest, collection fees, card surcharges, chargebacks, refunds and automatic debits are not authorized by this policy. Use only reviewed agreed terms and transaction-specific approvals.

## Refunds and credits

Record reason, original invoice/transaction, refundable amount, tax adjustment, funding route and client communication. Owner approves policy eligibility and exact action; separate final transaction confirmation is required before execution. Refund through the original approved processor route when appropriate. Never accept changed payment instructions solely from an email; independently verify through a known contact/channel. Maintain receipt and ledger reconciliation.

## Processor costs and disputes

Book gross collections, processor fees and net settlements separately. Reconcile refunds, chargebacks, holds, reserves and timing differences. Do not treat a processor balance as unrestricted cash. Preserve evidence for disputes, meet the processor deadline and involve the owner; do not submit inaccurate evidence or concede a claim automatically.

## Expenses and commissions

Require pre-approval for reimbursable/vendor spend with cost, payer, recurrence, terms and limit. Client-paid accounts are preferred when suitable. A provider-paid service requires approved resale/pass-through terms and cancellation exposure review. Pay commissions only under the reviewed written plan and payroll rules; no automatic wage deduction or clawback for a client refund.
