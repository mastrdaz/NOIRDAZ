# Monthly close and financial review

Version 0.1 · Prepared 2026-10-13 · Proposed operating practice pending owner adoption

## Close the books

- [ ] Obtain bank, card, processor, payroll and supplier statements securely
- [ ] Reconcile every account to statements; investigate differences and duplicates
- [ ] Reconcile gross sales, fees, refunds, chargebacks, reserves and net deposits
- [ ] Match invoices/credits/payments and age receivables; assign collection/dispute actions
- [ ] Match active subscriptions to recurring invoices and MRR bridge
- [ ] Categorize expenses; attach receipts and approved reimbursement evidence
- [ ] Allocate hosting/support costs consistently without double counting
- [ ] Review deposits/deferred revenue, prepaid costs, fixed assets and accruals with bookkeeper
- [ ] Reconcile payroll wages, taxes, benefits, commissions and outstanding liabilities
- [ ] Review contractor reporting eligibility with current-year CPA rules
- [ ] Verify sales/use tax, payroll filing/deposit and income-tax reserve status
- [ ] Lock/export the reviewed period and store backup privately

## Make decisions

Compare recognized revenue, delivery gross margin, fully loaded service contribution, operating result, AR, unrestricted cash and forecast obligations. Review the three largest cost overruns, unprofitable support clients, upcoming renewals and next 90 days of cash needs. Record price/scope/capacity decisions rather than changing client terms unilaterally.

Actions: [[FINANCE_ACTIONS]] with owner and due. Unreconciled balance [[OPEN_RECONCILIATIONS]]. Owner signoff [[CLOSE_SIGNOFF]]. Do not label an incomplete reconciliation “closed.”

## Annual records

Use a CPA/counsel-approved category retention schedule, including tax/supporting records, payroll, employment/I-9, contracts, invoices, incident and litigation records. IRS employment-tax records generally require at least four years; other periods vary, and legal holds override routine disposal. Do not use one blanket deletion date. Source and current qualifications: [IRS recordkeeping](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/how-long-should-i-keep-records), checked 2026-10-13; professional verification required before adopting a schedule.
