# Tax reserve guide

Version 0.1 · Prepared 2026-10-13 · Proposed operating practice pending owner adoption

A cash reserve is a planning control, not a tax calculation. No universal reserve percentage is approved. Entity classification, owner income, payroll, nexus and deductions materially change the result.

1. CPA confirms legal/tax entity, fiscal year, accounting method, elections, owner compensation/distributions and jurisdictions.
2. Build a rolling forecast of taxable income, withholding, credits and estimated payments using CPA-supplied assumptions. Keep sales/payroll taxes and income-tax estimates separate.
3. Set an owner-approved reserve amount and review frequency. Record the calculation date, assumptions, next payment and amount already held.
4. Compare each month against year-to-date results and pending liabilities; increase/reduce forecast only with evidence.
5. Before any payment or transfer, verify recipient, amount, form/period, due date, account and confirmation requirements. This guide does not authorize moving money.

Owner-level estimated taxes may differ from entity obligations. A calendar with quarterly review dates does not establish an actual deposit/filing schedule. Have the CPA verify federal/state estimated tax safe-harbor rules and dates for the applicable year, and payroll deposit schedules determined by actual liability/history.

Keep tax returns, notices and identifiers in restricted storage. Links and current tax obligations are maintained in [Wisconsin requirements](../compliance/WISCONSIN_REQUIREMENTS.md) and the [compliance calendar](../compliance/ANNUAL_COMPLIANCE_CALENDAR.md).
