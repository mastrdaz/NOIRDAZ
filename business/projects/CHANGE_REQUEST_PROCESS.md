# Change request process

Version 0.1 · Prepared 2026-10-13 · Proposed operating practice pending owner adoption

A request changes scope when it alters agreed deliverables, integrations, data, acceptance, support, dates, assumptions, client dependencies or price. Defect correction within agreed criteria is not automatically extra work.

1. Record request ID, requester, reason and current baseline. Acknowledge receipt without agreeing to cost or schedule.
2. Classify defect, clarification or scope change. Resolve ambiguity with the owner and client; do not use classification to avoid a contracted obligation.
3. Assess build/test/migration/security/privacy/support and vendor impacts, fees, tax review, dates, risks and alternatives.
4. Prepare the legal Change Order referencing exact SOW sections and versions. Include revised acceptance and any recurring cost.
5. Owner approves feasibility and economics; authorized client approves the written change. Purchases and external execution still require applicable approval.
6. Only then update plan, quote/invoice schedule, configuration and client record. Keep prior versions.
7. Verify changed behavior and close the request with evidence.

Emergency containment may use pre-authorized incident actions within the signed service scope. Record facts and obtain approval for additional scope/spend; urgency is not authority to purchase, transact or expand access.
