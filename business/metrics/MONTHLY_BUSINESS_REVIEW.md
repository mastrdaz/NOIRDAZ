## Scorecard

Record actual, prior period, target if adopted, variance reason and source for: leads/discovery/proposals/wins; conversion and sales cycle; MRR bridge and one-time revenue; gross margin and fully loaded service contribution; hosting/support cost; AR aging/unrestricted cash; active clients/churn/average MRR; tickets/backlog/health/renewals; uptime/data gaps/backup failures/incidents/overdue maintenance/vendor deadlines.

## Review checklist

- [ ] Monthly finance close reconciled or open items clearly labeled
- [ ] Pipeline next actions and realistic capacity reviewed
- [ ] Every unhealthy/unreviewed client has a next action
- [ ] Below-floor services have scope/cost/renewal decision, no unilateral contract change
- [ ] Backups restored in tests; missed checks and maintenance gaps assigned
- [ ] Access, vendor and legal/compliance deadlines reviewed
- [ ] Employee workload/training/payroll issues reviewed privately
- [ ] Three improvements at most selected for next month with measurable completion
