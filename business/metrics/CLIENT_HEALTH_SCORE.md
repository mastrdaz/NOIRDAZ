# Client health review

Version 0.1 · Prepared 2026-10-13 · Proposed operating practice pending owner adoption

Proposed internal prioritization aid, not a prediction or automatic client decision. Review monthly and after a material incident. Rate only from current evidence; missing information is “unreviewed.”

| Dimension | Weight | 0 high risk | 1 needs attention | 2 healthy |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| Delivery/service | 30% | Material unresolved failure | Workaround or repeated issues | Agreed outcomes met |
| Relationship | 20% | Sponsor unresolved concern | Slow/unclear decisions | Engaged authorized contact |
| Payment | 20% | Material overdue/disputed balance | Recent delays | Current under agreed terms |
| Economics | 20% | Below approved viable floor | Support/cost trend eroding margin | Sustainable within plan |
| Renewal fit | 10% | Exit expressed or major mismatch | Value/need unconfirmed | Need and review date confirmed |

Score = sum(weight × rating ÷ 2) × 100. If any dimension lacks evidence, do not calculate a final score; flag unreviewed. Proposed bands: 80–100 stable, 50–79 review, below 50 owner attention. Any critical outage/security/termination/payment dispute flag triggers owner review regardless of score.

Private record: client ID, review date, dimension ratings with evidence, score, override reason, open concern, next action, owner and due. Do not infer personal traits or use sensitive personal information. The purpose is to improve service, resolve disputes and plan capacity; it does not authorize contact, suspension, pricing changes or termination.
