# Sales talking points

Version 0.1 · Prepared 2026-10-13 · Proposed operating practice pending owner adoption

Use facts from the engagement. Avoid unsupported certifications, uptime, savings, legal or payment-compliance claims.

- Open: “What is taking too much time or causing mistakes today?”
- Explain: “We build and support practical websites and software. We first define the outcome, the scope and who will own the accounts.”
- Hosting: Explain the actual included maintenance, backup and support scope. Distinguish monitoring from continuous human coverage.
- Costs: Explain setup versus recurring costs, third-party charges and what triggers a change order. Present the approved quote rather than inventing a discount.
- Ownership: Identify client-owned domains, data and merchant accounts; explain deliverable and reusable-component licensing under the reviewed agreement.
- Unknowns: “I need to verify that before promising it.” Record an owner and date for the answer.
- Close: Agree one useful next step and a decision date. Do not create urgency or imply approval.

Sales cannot sign contracts, alter liability/IP, offer an SLA, spend money, issue credits, promise custom features or change prices without the owner's approval. Route regulated data, card storage, unsupported integrations and unusual insurance requirements to the owner before proposal.
