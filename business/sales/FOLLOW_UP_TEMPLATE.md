# Sales follow up templates

Version 0.1 · Prepared 2026-10-13 · Proposed operating practice pending owner adoption

Each is a draft requiring appropriate sending approval. Fill context and remove brackets. Stop when declined, opted out or another date is agreed.

## After discovery

To [[CLIENT_CONTACT]] · Subject [[PROJECT_NAME]] next steps

Thanks for walking through [[BUSINESS_OBJECTIVE]]. I captured [[KEY_SCOPE_POINTS]]. We still need [[OPEN_QUESTIONS]] before finalizing scope. The next step is [[NEXT_ACTION]] by [[NEXT_DUE]]. Please correct anything I missed.

## Quote check in

To [[CLIENT_CONTACT]] · Subject [[QUOTE_ID]] review

Have you had a chance to review the quote for [[PROJECT_NAME]]? The main decision is [[CLIENT_DECISION_NEEDED]]. I can clarify [[KNOWN_OPEN_POINT]]. The current quote is valid through [[QUOTE_EXPIRY_DATE]].

## Close the loop

To [[CLIENT_CONTACT]] · Subject [[PROJECT_NAME]] timing

I haven't heard back, so I'll close this opportunity for now. If the timing changes, we can revisit scope, availability and pricing then.

Suggested cadence: first follow-up 3–5 business days after delivery, one more after 7–10 business days, then close or defer after roughly 14–21 days when appropriate. These are internal guidelines; relationship context and an agreed date take priority. Do not activate an automated sequence from this document.
