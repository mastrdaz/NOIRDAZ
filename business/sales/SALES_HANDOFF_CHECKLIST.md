# Sales handoff checklist

Version 0.1 · Prepared 2026-10-13 · Proposed operating practice pending owner adoption

Opportunity [[LEAD_ID]] · Client [[CLIENT_ID]] · Sales owner [[SALES_OWNER]] · Delivery owner [[DELIVERY_OWNER]]

- [ ] Correct executed legal names and authorized signers verified
- [ ] Signed agreement/SOW/addenda and approved redlines stored privately
- [ ] Exact deliverables, acceptance, exclusions and milestones reviewed together
- [ ] Deposit/credit exception and billing owner confirmed
- [ ] Setup and recurring prices, vendor costs, limits and renewal notices recorded
- [ ] Promises made in sales copied into scope or explicitly resolved with client
- [ ] Client assets, account ownership and dependency deadlines assigned
- [ ] Data/payment/security/insurance risks and decisions passed to delivery
- [ ] Support expectations match actual staffing and signed entitlement
- [ ] Kickoff date, first deliverable and next owner/due confirmed

Handoff accepted by [[DELIVERY_OWNER]] on [[HANDOFF_DATE]]. Open exceptions: [[HANDOFF_EXCEPTIONS]]. Sales Won does not equal launched or billable recurring service.
