# Sales pipeline

Version 0.1 · Prepared 2026-10-13 · Proposed operating practice pending owner adoption

Use one opportunity per proposed scope. Each record has next action, owner and due date. Follow-up times below are internal suggestions, not automated sends or client promises. Respect consent, channel norms and opt-outs.

| Stage | Entry | Exit | Required notes | Follow-up guidance |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| New Lead | Contact captured and source recorded | Legitimate channel and next contact assigned | Need, source, consent/context | Review in 2 business days |
| Contacted | First contact logged | Fit questions answered or discovery requested | Response, constraints, next contact | Follow up after 3–5 business days |
| Qualified | Owner confirms service/budget/risk fit | Discovery date and participants agreed | Budget band, authority, timing, risk | Schedule within agreed timeline |
| Discovery Scheduled | Calendar agreed | Call held or rescheduled | Agenda, client questions, attendees | Reminder 1 business day before if appropriate |
| Discovery Complete | Notes and dependencies captured | Owner approves solution/proposal path | Success criteria, systems, risks, approvals | Send recap in 2 business days |
| Demo/Proposal | Feasible approach presented | Option selected and scope stable enough to cost | Feedback, objections, exclusions | Check in 3–5 business days |
| Quote Sent | Owner-approved quote delivered | Written direction to contract or revise | Version, expiry, tax assumptions, recipient | Check in 3–5 days then once near expiry |
| Negotiation | Specific open commercial/legal issue | Approved terms resolved or opportunity closed | Redlines, authority, concession cost | Every agreed decision date |
| Won | Both parties executed required contract | Funding gate and onboarding owner assigned | Signed versions, commercial terms, start gates | Handoff within 1 business day |
| Lost | Client decline, no fit or owner close decision | Reason and future contact permission recorded | Lost reason, lessons, avoid blame | No sequence after opt-out |
| Onboarding | Executed deal handed to delivery | Client onboarding checklist complete | Assets, access, deposit, blockers | Weekly or faster when blocked |
| Active Client | Contracted service/project active | Renewal, expansion or offboarding event | Health, support, margin, renewal dates | Monthly health review |

## Lost reasons

Budget below viable cost; timing deferred; no decision; lost to another provider; internal solution; service mismatch; risk/compliance mismatch; no authorized sponsor; scope not agreed; client withdrew; other with explanation. Distinguish a lost deal from a deferred opportunity. Do not keep forecast revenue for either.

## Weekly pipeline review

- [ ] Remove stale values, duplicate opportunities and unsupported win probabilities
- [ ] Review overdue next actions and quote expirations
- [ ] Verify proposed start dates against real capacity
- [ ] Resolve legal/data/payment risks before promising scope
- [ ] Count closed outcomes in the period separately from open pipeline
