Version 0.1 · Prepared 2026-10-13 · Proposed operating practice pending owner adoption

## Before work begins

- [ ] Verify legal party, authorized signer and billing contact
- [ ] Record signed MSA/SOW and applicable service/privacy/payment exhibits
- [ ] Confirm scope, exclusions, criteria, milestones, support and client dependencies
- [ ] Verify any required deposit has cleared; record approved exception if lawful
- [ ] Approve third-party spend, payer, recurring terms and procurement separately
- [ ] Create restricted client workspace and contract/invoice indexes
- [ ] Complete data/privacy and security review; stop for unresolved regulated scope
- [ ] Confirm insurance coverage and certificate obligations with broker when triggered

## Kickoff and access

- [ ] Assign owner and backup; confirm communication and escalation channels
- [ ] Schedule kickoff, review dates and client asset deadlines
- [ ] Inventory accounts; verify client domain and merchant ownership
- [ ] Grant named least-privilege access through secure invitation; require MFA
- [ ] Separate production from test; use synthetic/redacted test data
- [ ] Capture current system/DNS backup and transfer constraints without changes
- [ ] Approve project plan and record next action
