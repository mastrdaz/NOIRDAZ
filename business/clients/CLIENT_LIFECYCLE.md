# Client lifecycle

Version 0.1 · Prepared 2026-10-13 · Proposed operating practice pending owner adoption

One private client record owns the current stage, next action, accountable person and due date. Sales pipeline labels map to this lifecycle; do not create two conflicting statuses. Record stage entry/exit dates, contract version, commercial amounts and evidence links privately.

| Stage | Required work and record | Gate to advance | Next owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lead | Record source, organization, contact permission, need, next contact | Contact channel appropriate; no sensitive data collected unnecessarily | Owner or sales assignee |
| Qualification | Fit, budget range, decision-maker, urgency, regulated-data/payment risks | Owner accepts discovery or records disqualification reason | Owner |
| Discovery | Complete notes, systems, outcomes, dependencies, risks | Client confirms material facts; unresolved risks have owners | Delivery lead |
| Proposal | Describe approach, scope choices, exclusions, capacity and assumptions | Internal feasibility/security review and client direction | Owner |
| Quote | Cost model, price, taxes for review, expiry, term, third-party costs | Owner approves economics; client selects option | Owner |
| Contract and SOW | Correct legal names, approved templates, negotiated changes, signatures | Both authorized signers execute complete agreement and required addenda | Owner |
| Deposit and payment setup | Approved invoice, cleared deposit if required, billing authority | Contract funding condition met; no payment credentials in records | Owner/bookkeeper |
| Access and intake | Contacts, assets, vault references, consent, environment ownership | Onboarding and project security checks passed | Delivery lead |
| Project execution | Plan, tasks, time/cost log, weekly status and risk record | Deliverables tested against agreed acceptance criteria | Delivery lead |
| Client review | Versioned review package, tests, defects, response deadline | Material defects resolved or documented exception approved | Client approver |
| Acceptance | Written acceptance with exact version and known issues | Authorized client acceptance; no inferred silence unless counsel-approved clause applies | Owner |
| Launch | Separate go/no-go, backup/rollback, maintenance window, launch authorization | Client production authorization and internal launch checklist complete | Delivery lead |
| Recurring service | Record actual start, entitlement, invoice schedule, recovery targets | Contract service start condition met and monitoring/support handoff confirmed | Service owner |
| Support | Triage, diagnose, communicate, restore, record time | Client informed of resolution or workaround; follow-up owned | Support assignee |
| Renewal or expansion | Review 90/60/30 days before contract notice/renewal dates where practicable | New term/price/scope approved under agreement; no silent entitlement changes | Owner |
| Offboarding | Effective termination, exports, transfer, revocation, billing, lawful retention | Receipt/transfer evidence and deletion schedule documented | Owner |

## Every handoff

- [ ] Stage, entry date, next action and due date are current
- [ ] One person owns the next action and knows the client contact
- [ ] Required approvals are linked to the exact scope/version
- [ ] Costs, dependencies, access, known issues and promises were handed over
- [ ] A blocked stage includes reason, decision-maker and escalation date
- [ ] Sensitive records remain private; dashboard uses client IDs only

Weekly, review all open records. A missing next action or a passed due date is an exception to resolve. Do not start uncontracted work, take production access or activate billing simply because a deal is marked Won.
