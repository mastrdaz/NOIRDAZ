Complete in restricted storage. Do not ask the client to send passwords, card details, bank data, tax identifiers or sensitive production exports.

- Services requested / budget range / desired date / hard deadline reason: [[SERVICES_REQUESTED]] / [[BUDGET_RANGE]] / [[TARGET_DATE]] / [[DEADLINE_REASON]]
- Existing website, provider and contract exit restrictions: [[CURRENT_SYSTEMS]]

- Open questions / owner / due: [[OPEN_QUESTIONS]]

Gate: owner reviews fit and risks, completes the privacy questionnaire when data is involved, and records whether discovery may proceed. A request for regulated-data services or card storage pauses quoting until specialist scope and safeguards are established.
