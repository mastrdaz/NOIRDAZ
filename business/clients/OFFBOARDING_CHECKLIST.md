# Client offboarding checklist

Version 0.1 · Prepared 2026-10-13 · Proposed operating practice pending owner adoption

Instance: [[CLIENT_ID]] · Owner [[RECORD_OWNER]] · Effective end [[TERMINATION_DATE]]

- [ ] Verify cancellation authority, contract notice and service end date
- [ ] Acknowledge exact scope ending; distinguish hosting, email, domains and support
- [ ] Identify business continuity risks, lawful retention and legal holds
- [ ] Agree export format, secure destination, recipient, timeline and authorized fees
- [ ] Follow hosting transfer runbook; test export completeness and receipt
- [ ] Transfer domain/DNS/account control by separate approved change, never casually disable it
- [ ] Deliver agreed source, database, configuration, docs and license inventory
- [ ] Revoke Noirdaz/employee/vendor access after transfer; rotate shared credentials securely
- [ ] Reconcile final invoice, prepaid amounts, credits, usage and disputed items
- [ ] Cancel authorized billing/vendor renewals without terminating retained client services
- [ ] Record backup aging/deletion, exceptions and legal holds; verify deletion where feasible
- [ ] Archive execution and accounting records under the approved retention schedule
- [ ] Record receipt, unresolved claims, completion evidence and last client contact

Owner signoff: [[OFFBOARDING_SIGNOFF]]. A final balance does not automatically authorize withholding client-owned data or domain access; follow the agreement and counsel advice. Never promise immediate deletion from immutable backups when the approved retention mechanism is expiry.
