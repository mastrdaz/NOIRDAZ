# Access revocation checklist

Version 0.1 · Prepared 2026-10-13 · Proposed operating practice pending owner adoption

Private case [[OFFBOARDING_ID]] · Person/vendor [[SUBJECT_ID]] · Owner [[RECORD_OWNER]] · Effective time [[REVOCATION_TIME]]

Plan with HR/counsel for employee departures and with the client for service transfers. Avoid deleting needed records or client-owned accounts.

- [ ] Inventory identity/email, password vault, Git, cloud/hosting, registrar/DNS, CRM, finance, support, analytics and client accounts
- [ ] Transfer ownership of required work/resources and approve continuity access
- [ ] Preserve legal holds, required logs and employment/business records
- [ ] Revoke sessions, app/OAuth grants, SSH/API keys, service access and device trust as authorized
- [ ] Disable named accounts at the approved time; do not merely remove a mailing list
- [ ] Rotate secrets the departing person knew or could export; verify dependents
- [ ] Recover equipment, keys and tokens through documented process
- [ ] Remove forwarding/delegation rules and unneeded third-party memberships
- [ ] Verify access denial and audit logs; record each system's evidence
- [ ] Set lawful retention/deletion schedule and unresolved exceptions

Verifier [[ACCESS_REVIEWER]] · Completion [[REVOCATION_COMPLETED_AT]] · Exceptions [[ACCESS_EXCEPTIONS]]. Permanent deletion, changed security settings and financial-account actions remain subject to their separate authorization rules.
