# Clause library for negotiation

DRAFT — ATTORNEY REVIEW — ALTERNATIVES ONLY

These alternatives are outside the operative templates. They are not cumulative and must not be pasted into a signing package without owner selection, counsel revision and reconciliation of cross-references. The base MSA remains a proposed draft, not an approved fallback. No amount, period, legal forum or liability allocation is selected here.

Each entry identifies the decision, sample operative text and integration risk. References are to the supplied MSA. Keep the discussion and risk notes outside the signed contract.

## CL01 Fixed term with signed renewal

**Decision:** Keep the base model's deliberate renewal rather than add automatic renewal. Relevant owner decision: OD18.

**Proposed text:** “This SOW expires on [[SERVICE_END_DATE]]. The parties may extend it only by a written renewal signed by their authorized representatives, identifying the new term, services and fees. Continued discussion, an unanswered renewal notice or an invoice sent after expiration does not itself create a new fixed term. Before expiration the parties will agree any temporary transition service in writing.”

**Integration:** Align scheduled shutdown, data export and billing. Avoid an unintended service interruption while waiting for signatures.

## CL02 Monthly ongoing service with cancellation

**Decision:** Consider an ongoing monthly service instead of repeated long fixed terms. Counsel must assess the actual renewal/cancellation rules; a monthly label is not an exemption. Relevant decisions: OD18–19.

**Proposed text:** “Service begins on [[RECURRING_SERVICE_START_EVENT]] and continues in monthly periods until terminated. Either party may end service by giving [[MONTHLY_CANCELLATION_NOTICE_DAYS]] days' written notice through [[CANCELLATION_METHOD]]. The effective end date is determined under [[MONTHLY_END_DATE_RULE]]. The parties will reconcile fees and any unused prepaid portion under [[MONTHLY_PRORATION_RULE]]. No cancellation fee applies except an approved third-party commitment specifically identified in the SOW. A price change requires [[RENEWAL_PRICE_CHANGE_PROCESS]] and preserves the Client cancellation right stated there.”

**Integration:** Fill the notice period, effective-date rule and proration explicitly. Align vendor terms and exit windows. Do not imply that longer notice is automatically permissible.

## CL03 Fixed automatic renewal with disclosure

**Decision:** Add automatic renewal only if the owner can operate legally required disclosure and reminder steps. Relevant decision: OD18.

**Proposed text:** “Unless either party declines renewal through [[CANCELLATION_METHOD]] by [[NONRENEWAL_DEADLINE_RULE]], this SOW renews for [[RENEWAL_PERIOD]]. Charges for the renewal are [[RENEWAL_PRICE_TERMS]]. Provider will give Client the conspicuous disclosures and separate acknowledgment, signature or initials required by applicable law and the notice described in [[RENEWAL_NOTICE_PROCESS]]. That process states the reminder window, authorized delivery method, price-change disclosure and delivery evidence. Failure to give a required notice has the consequence required by applicable law. Nothing in this clause restricts a mandatory cancellation right.”

**Integration:** Counsel must build the actual disclosure/acknowledgment form and statutory reminders into the executed package. This sample is not itself a verified Wisconsin § 134.49 compliance form. Do not combine it with CL01 or inconsistent monthly cancellation language.

## CL04 Written acceptance after a final reminder

**Decision:** Retain express acceptance but give the stalled-review process a definite next step. Relevant decision: OD08.

**Proposed text:** “If Client does not provide acceptance or a specific rejection within the review period, Provider will send a reminder identifying the deliverable, criteria and a proposed review meeting. If review remains outstanding after [[REVIEW_ESCALATION_DAYS]] business days, the parties' commercial approvers will agree a completion or termination plan. Provider may pause affected work on written notice while preserving delivered work. No acceptance, launch authority or additional fee arises solely from silence.”

**Integration:** Coordinate with milestones and termination. If the owner instead requests deemed acceptance, counsel should draft it specifically, define meaningful delivery and notice, exclude latent defects and regulated release approvals, and reconcile it with the acceptance form. No deemed-acceptance clause is preselected in this library.

## CL05 Product license instead of assignment

**Decision:** Use for Provider-owned products or reusable platforms, rather than falsely assigning them as custom deliverables. Relevant decisions: OD10–11 and OD30.

**Proposed text:** “The Product identified in [[SUBSCRIPTION_PRODUCTS]] remains owned by Provider or its licensors. During the paid subscription term, Provider grants Client a nonexclusive right to access and use the Product for the users and purposes stated in [[PRODUCT_LICENSE_ATTACHMENT]]. Client retains Client Data. Provider will provide the export described in [[EXIT_AND_RETENTION_SCHEDULE]] on expiration or termination. Any separately identified Assigned Deliverables remain subject to MSA Section 6.3. This license does not authorize Provider to sell Client Data or use it for unrelated model training.”

**Integration:** The referenced product terms still need to address user limits, suspension, service changes, third-party rights, portability and continuity. Do not use this paragraph as complete SaaS terms.

## CL06 Portfolio permission

**Decision:** Optional, narrow publicity permission after the client approves the specific use. Relevant decision: OD31.

**Proposed text:** “Client permits Provider to use only the assets and factual description in [[APPROVED_PUBLICITY_MATERIALS]], solely in [[APPROVED_PUBLICITY_CHANNELS]], beginning [[PUBLICITY_START_DATE]]. Provider will not disclose nonpublic data, performance metrics, source code or security information. Material edits and new channels require Client's written approval. Client may withdraw future use by written notice, and Provider will remove the materials within [[PUBLICITY_REMOVAL_DAYS]] days from channels it controls, excluding archival copies retained solely for legal records.”

**Integration:** Confirm trademark and image rights. A completed project does not imply endorsement or permission to identify the client.

## CL07 Paid transition beyond the included export

**Decision:** Separate included return of Client Data from additional migration labor. Relevant decisions: OD19 and OD27.

**Proposed text:** “The fee for the ordinary export in [[INCLUDED_EXIT_EXPORT]] is included in the service fees already agreed. At Client's request, Provider will perform the additional migration services in [[ADDITIONAL_EXIT_WORK]] for [[EXIT_WORK_FEES]], subject to the approved schedule and ceiling. No extra labor begins until both parties approve its scope and fee. A fee dispute does not transfer ownership of Client Data or domains and does not authorize withholding an export that the MSA requires Provider to supply.”

**Integration:** Document formats, delivery window, verification, system compatibility and third-party portability limits. Do not promise free custom conversion as part of a routine export.

## CL08 Liability cap architecture

**Decision:** Select a comprehensible cap with deliberate exceptions. This is a framework for counsel to complete, not a suggested number. Relevant decisions: OD13–16.

**Proposed text:** “For claims allocated to the general category, each party's aggregate liability is capped at [[GENERAL_LIABILITY_CAP]]. The elevated categories and their respective caps are [[ELEVATED_LIABILITY_CAPS]]. The categories not subject to a contractual cap are [[UNCAPPED_CLAIM_CATEGORIES]]. Related claims aggregate under [[CLAIM_AGGREGATION_RULE]], and defense expenses are treated under [[DEFENSE_COST_CAP_TREATMENT]]. A claim falling in multiple categories receives [[OVERLAPPING_CAP_RULE]]; the caps are not cumulative unless that rule expressly states otherwise. Liability that cannot lawfully be limited remains unaffected.”

**Integration:** Decide a fee-based formula, fixed amount, combined floor or other method only after pricing and insurance review. Specify paid versus payable fees, included/excluded pass-through costs, time period, currency and all-SOW versus affected-SOW scope. Prevent accidental unlimited liability and a cap that becomes zero in a new relationship.

## CL09 Specific treatment of restoration and incident costs

**Decision:** Decide whether identified costs are recoverable direct losses and which cap applies, rather than rely on ambiguous labels. Relevant decisions: OD14–16 and OD26.

**Proposed text:** “Reasonable, documented costs of restoring affected Client Data and responding to a Security Incident are recoverable only to the extent caused by a party's breach and to the extent stated in [[DAMAGES_EXCLUSION_EXCEPTIONS]]. Those costs are subject to the applicable cap in Section 11. Recoverable response categories, exclusions, mitigation duties and any consent requirement are stated in that schedule. A legally required urgent notice will not be delayed solely while the parties resolve cost allocation.”

**Integration:** Specify forensic work, legal advice, notices, monitoring, regulatory proceedings and third-party demands separately. Coordinate insurer approval and privilege. Avoid double recovery through indemnity and direct claims.

## CL10 Limited service credit

**Decision:** Use only with a measurable, operationally supported SLA. Relevant decision: OD22.

**Proposed text:** “For failure of the availability commitment identified in [[SLA_EXHIBIT_REFERENCE]], Client may request the credit calculated under [[SLA_CREDIT_FORMULA]] using the agreed measurement and claim procedure. Credits apply to the affected recurring service and do not exceed [[SLA_CREDIT_CEILING]] for the measurement period. Credits do not release claims for a separate breach of confidentiality, security, intellectual property or another obligation. Repeated qualifying failures give Client the termination right in [[SLA_REPEATED_FAILURE_REMEDY]].”

**Integration:** Define monitoring source, time denominator, excluded events and evidence; choose whether credits are the exclusive remedy for the availability failure itself. Do not insert “sole remedy for all services” casually. No uptime percentage is supplied here.

## CL11 Subprocessor notice and objection

**Decision:** Choose a manageable vendor-change process. Relevant decision: OD25.

**Proposed text:** “Provider will give [[SUBPROCESSOR_NOTICE_DAYS]] days' notice of a proposed new subprocessor with access to Client Data, identifying its function, relevant locations and data categories. Client may object within [[SUBPROCESSOR_OBJECTION_DAYS]] days on a documented, reasonable privacy or security ground. The parties will discuss an alternative or appropriate safeguards. If no reasonable solution is agreed, either party may terminate the affected processing before the new subprocessor receives the data, with a refund of unused prepaid services. Existing confidentiality and security duties continue during transition.”

**Integration:** Provide a lawful urgent-replacement procedure if necessary and reconcile data-processing law, named vendors, geographic restrictions and upstream notice rights. A vendor list on a mutable webpage alone is not a complete process.

## CL12 Legal hold and backup expiration

**Decision:** Use bounded retention rather than a blanket deletion promise or indefinite retention exception. Relevant decision: OD27.

**Proposed text:** “A specific legal hold or mandatory recordkeeping obligation suspends ordinary deletion only for records reasonably within its scope. The retaining party will identify a responsible custodian, restrict access, record the basis and review the need for continued retention. Where lawful, it will tell the other party the affected categories and expected process for release. Routine backup copies will expire through [[BACKUP_EXPIRATION_SCHEDULE]] and will not be restored for ordinary use; if restored for disaster recovery, the agreed deletion restrictions will be reapplied. Protected duties continue until the records are deleted.”

**Integration:** Counsel and the CPA should identify the actual retention duties. Do not promise immediate selective deletion from immutable backups if the system cannot deliver it.

## Using this library

Record the selected clause ID, full approved wording, affected section, approving owner and counsel, and date in the contract change history. Delete competing alternatives from the signing package. Recheck every repeated value in the SOW, addenda, operations checklists, quote and invoices. No library clause has legal effect merely because its file is present in a repository.
