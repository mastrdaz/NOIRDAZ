# NOIRDAZ legal document system

DRAFT — ATTORNEY REVIEW — NOT APPROVED FOR CLIENT USE

Prepared for the internal business operating system on 2026-10-13. This suite supplies substantive starting drafts for a small software, website, hosting and managed-services business. It is ready to give counsel for review, not to send for signature. NOIRDAZ INDUSTRIES is the project brand; no legal entity, LLC status, registered address, insurance coverage or authority to contract has been established here.

## What to review first

1. Read [Owner decisions](review/OWNER_DECISIONS_REGISTER.md) and resolve the business choices with the owner.
2. Review the [Master Services Agreement](templates/MASTER_SERVICES_AGREEMENT.md), particularly Sections 6 and 8–14 and the uncompleted risk schedule.
3. Use the [Attorney review checklist](review/ATTORNEY_REVIEW_CHECKLIST.md) to request whatever facts, documents or changes counsel needs.
4. Select the appropriate project and service documents using the [agreement decision tree](AGREEMENT_DECISION_TREE.md).
5. Complete the [placeholder reference](PLACEHOLDER_REFERENCE.md) for that package; all applicable fields need definite values or an expressly appropriate “None.”

All operative language is proposed drafting, including language that is already written without brackets. No default in this suite is an owner-approved legal position. Amounts, time periods, commercial commitments, legal selections and risk limits remain open where indicated. Missing fields must not be treated as zero, unlimited, waived or approved.

## Contract architecture

Use one MSA per legal client relationship, then a generic SOW or website build order for a project. Add only the service addenda actually needed. The website agreement is not an independent short-form substitute for the MSA. A hosting or support-only relationship still needs an SOW defining service, fees, term and start event.

The MSA controls unless a signed amendment or expressly identified override says otherwise. A data processing agreement governs its processing subject matter. A change order changes only identified provisions. Proposals, internal policies, pricing calculators, marketing claims and vendor webpages are not silently incorporated. Preserve an immutable attachment index with titles, versions and dates in every signed package.

The MSA uses express written acceptance, separates launch approval, excludes automatic renewal unless deliberately added, and assigns only identified custom work after payment allocated to that work. Those are proposed business positions requiring approval. They are not conclusions that another approach would be unlawful.

## Review materials

[Clause library](CLAUSE_LIBRARY.md) contains optional replacement language and negotiation choices. Keep it outside a client signing package unless counsel selects and integrates a clause. [Legal research notes](review/LEGAL_RESEARCH_SOURCES.md) contain dated official sources and verification limits. [Quality review](review/QUALITY_REVIEW.md) records structural checks, not legal approval.

A DPA, BAA, SaaS end-user terms, consumer subscription terms, privacy notice, employee/contractor IP agreement, commission agreement and public-sector procurement rider are not provided as ready-made substitutes in this suite. The decision tree identifies when they need separate drafting. Do not extend the MSA into those uses by changing only the title.

## Before signature

- Verify both legal parties and each signatory's authority
- Complete every applicable placeholder and attached schedule
- Resolve liability, indemnity, warranty, renewal, privacy, IP and governing-law decisions
- Match promises to actual staffing, architecture, backup tests, vendors and insurance
- Confirm counsel's changes were incorporated consistently across all documents
- Separate attorney/owner review notes from the client package
- Remove draft status only after the owner and counsel authorize the finalized version
- Record the signed version, attachments, start conditions and next renewal/cancellation action

Do not place live secrets or unnecessary personal information in these files. An attorney may need additional facts and documents and may substantially change the drafts. No claim is made that any provision is valid, enforceable, suitable for every client or sufficient for regulatory compliance.
