# Attorney review checklist

DRAFT — REVIEW REQUEST — NO LEGAL CONCLUSIONS

Prepared 2026-10-13 for counsel reviewing the NOIRDAZ services templates. Please revise the proposed positions, identify missing agreements and request whatever information is needed. The intended business is small, Wisconsin-first, with one owner and up to five employees; entity formation, rates, staffing commitments and insurance remain unverified.

## 1 Intake and legal identity

- [ ] Obtain actual formation/registration or sole-proprietor facts, DBA usage, addresses and signatory authority
- [ ] Confirm customer entity and business-to-business versus consumer, government, education or regulated status
- [ ] Identify states/countries of parties, performance, workers and affected individuals
- [ ] Review existing customer contracts, promises, planned Wisconsin Surplus work and any prior template commitments rather than assuming replacement
- [ ] Confirm whether services, licensed software, goods, mixed contracts or product subscriptions require different treatment

## 2 Formation and document hierarchy

- [ ] Review MSA/SOW/addendum precedence and requirement to expressly identify overrides
- [ ] Verify proposal/quote, purchase-order, portal and online-policy treatment
- [ ] Confirm website agreement's role as an SOW and avoid duplicate orders for the same work
- [ ] Assess signature method, electronic records, authority, counterparts and retention of exact attachments
- [ ] Confirm governing law, venue, escalation, remedies, assignment and any public-sector restrictions
- [ ] Decide whether a shorter form or a separate consumer/SaaS/public-sector agreement is needed

## 3 Fees acceptance and termination

- [ ] Review taxes, deposits, earned versus unearned fees, late charges, surcharges, collection costs and refunds
- [ ] Confirm clear treatment of good-faith disputes, undisputed payment, suspension notice, cure and safe data access
- [ ] Test acceptance criteria, review period, correction and failed-cure remedies; consider latent defects and stalled review
- [ ] Confirm launch authority is separate from acceptance and cannot silently authorize financial transactions
- [ ] Review convenience termination, noncancelable commitments, mitigation, refund allocation and any proposed fee acceleration
- [ ] Review bankruptcy/insolvency wording and force-majeure limitations against applicable law

## 4 Intellectual property and warranties

- [ ] Verify employee/contractor rights chain and a signed assignment sufficient for the actual rights transferred
- [ ] Review custom work, Background Materials, open-source, third-party and product-license boundaries
- [ ] Confirm paid-deliverable rights and source delivery work on early termination and partial payments
- [ ] Review copyleft/source-disclosure risks, license inventory and third-party subscriptions
- [ ] Review express/implied warranties, conspicuousness, disclaimer limits, warranty duration, cure and exclusive-remedy proposals
- [ ] Separate a tested accessibility specification from any legal-compliance representation
- [ ] Confirm no unapproved publicity, trademark or general AI-training rights arise

## 5 Indemnity limitation and insurance

- [ ] Complete cap formula, currency, fee base, lookback, aggregation across claims/SOWs and defense-cost treatment
- [ ] Address low/no fees, multiple SOWs, related incidents and post-termination claims
- [ ] Decide general, elevated and uncapped categories separately; review nonwaivable liabilities
- [ ] Classify restoration, security response, loss of data, lost profits, third-party awards and amounts owed
- [ ] Review mutual IP indemnity, exclusions, territory, patents, client-material rights and remedies
- [ ] Decide any additional privacy/security, bodily injury/property, regulatory or other indemnities
- [ ] Review notice, defense control, conflicts, settlement consent and failure-to-defend consequences
- [ ] Match obligations to actual policies, exclusions, claims-made/tail issues, subcontractors and incident consent requirements with the broker
- [ ] Evaluate applicable state requirements for limitations, exculpation, negligence, indemnification and damages exclusions; do not assume standard wording is enforceable

## 6 Renewal and recurring services

- [ ] Confirm applicability and current text of Wisconsin Stat. § 134.49 and any exemptions, disclosure, signature/initial, reminder, timing and delivery requirements
- [ ] Check other applicable state B2B and consumer renewal/cancellation regimes
- [ ] Verify current federal negative-option requirements rather than relying on superseded 2024 summaries
- [ ] Separate contractual renewal, service term, recurring invoice interval and payment-method authorization
- [ ] Make cancellation method and effective date operationally clear; review price changes and refunds
- [ ] Ensure any SLA defines measurements, exclusions, credit calculation, claim procedure and repeated-failure remedies
- [ ] Check that a service credit does not unintentionally become the sole remedy for unrelated serious breaches

## 7 Privacy security and regulated work

- [ ] Review the completed questionnaire, actual data map, locations, vendors, instructions, retention and controls
- [ ] Determine parties' roles, applicable privacy laws and required DPA, BAA, international transfers or sector clauses
- [ ] Verify children, health, education, financial, biometrics and high-impact decision triggers; do not rely on a “no sensitive data” clause to erase actual facts
- [ ] Review subprocessors, flow-down obligations, notice/objection rights and exit if an objection cannot be resolved
- [ ] Reconcile contractual incident definition and notice deadline with all statutory, customer, processor and insurer deadlines
- [ ] Confirm allocation of external notices, preservation, investigation, cooperation and incident costs
- [ ] Review rights-request support, audit evidence, regulator requests and protections for other clients' data
- [ ] Distinguish production deletion, backup rotation, mandatory records and specific legal holds
- [ ] Confirm any security, certification or insurance statement is evidenced and within current operating capability

## 8 Payment and website specifics

- [ ] Verify merchant of record, account ownership, acquiring relationship and no accidental funds custody/payment-facilitator role
- [ ] Review hosted redirect/iframe versus direct-post or other card data flows; map all scripts, logs, tokens and webhooks
- [ ] Confirm SAQ/validation path with the compliance-accepting entity and allocate ASV scanning and applicable script protections
- [ ] Review live-test authority, idempotency, transaction reconciliation, refund/cancellation controls and recurring consent
- [ ] Confirm processor fees, disputes, token portability and offboarding are accurately described
- [ ] Distinguish public-sector Title II web rules from private-business obligations; verify the 2026 deadline changes where relevant
- [ ] Require additional terms for digital products, platform marketplace features, lending, stored value or other specialized models

## 9 Final package gate

- [ ] Approve owner decisions and record deviations from the standard template
- [ ] Complete all applicable placeholders and attach actual referenced schedules; remove unused clauses deliberately
- [ ] Resolve contradictory fees, dates, response windows, remedies or policy references
- [ ] Obtain owner, CPA and insurer input for commercial/tax/coverage matters outside counsel's factual knowledge
- [ ] Confirm the exact final documents and attachments, permitted signatories and signing order
- [ ] Record counsel's review date, reviewer, scope, remaining limits and the next review trigger
- [ ] Remove DRAFT status only after the specific package is approved; retain the reviewed original and change history

The checklist is a review aid, not a representation that satisfying every item establishes validity or compliance. Counsel may identify additional issues based on the actual work and parties.
