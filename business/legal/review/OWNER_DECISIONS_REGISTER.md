# Owner decisions register

Prepared 2026-10-13. The following choices belong to the business owner, with counsel and insurance/CPA input where indicated. They are intentionally separate from operative contract prose. All items are open unless a dated decision record is added. Written language already present in a draft is a proposal, not evidence of approval.

For each decision record: selected position; rationale; financial/operational effect; counsel response; approver; date; template version; and exceptions allowed. Counsel may request any supporting facts or substitute a different structure.

## Suggested review sequence

First settle OD01–03 and the standard commercial model. Then review OD08–19 together because acceptance, IP, warranty, indemnity, liability and termination affect each other. Review hosting/support and data commitments against actual operating capacity. Send counsel the proposed decisions and source material, leaving disputed items visibly open. Do not remove open issues to make a signing packet look finished.
