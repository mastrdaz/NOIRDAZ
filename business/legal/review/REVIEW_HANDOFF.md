# Noirdaz attorney review guide

DRAFT ATTORNEY REVIEW

Prepared October 13 2026 | Version 0.1

This packet is intended to let counsel review and revise a substantive proposed contract system for a small Wisconsin-first software, website, hosting and managed-services business. Noirdaz is a working brand here. Exact entity status, contracting identity, authority, address, commercial terms and insurance remain to be established. The owner wants polished review drafts and expects counsel to request any additional facts or documents needed.

## Packet contents

The editable contracts are the Master Services Agreement, Statement of Work, Managed Hosting Addendum, Website Build Agreement, Software and Integration Addendum, Payment Integration Addendum, Mutual NDA, Change Order, Acceptance and Launch Authorization, Support Policy Exhibit, Offboarding Agreement and Transfer Checklist, and Privacy and Data Questionnaire. The questionnaire collects facts and is not a processing contract.

The source folder also includes the agreement decision tree, clause alternatives, a complete legal placeholder reference, 32-item owner decision register, attorney review checklist and dated official-source notes. Keep those review materials separate from a final client signing package.

## Proposed architecture

Use one MSA with a project-specific SOW or the website order. Attach only applicable service exhibits. The MSA establishes precedence and permits deliberate identified overrides; the Change Order modifies identified SOW provisions and requires a separate instrument for an MSA amendment. Preserve exact attachment titles, versions and dates.

Written acceptance and production launch are separate decisions. The proposed baseline does not treat silence, payment or use alone as acceptance and does not create automatic renewal without a deliberate executed provision. Identified custom work is assigned after payment of its allocated fee; background materials and third-party licenses remain separately identified. These are proposals for review, not approved legal positions.

## Decisions to resolve with the owner

- Verify parties, signer capacity, governing law, venue and standard commercial model.
- Set prices, deposit application, due dates, recurring start, expense authority, refund timing, lawful late charges and cancellation/renewal approach.
- Review acceptance/cure, warranties, IP assignment and reusable-component license as one connected allocation of delivery risk.
- Complete the liability schedule, caps, carveouts, damages exclusions, indemnity scope, defense control/cost treatment and remedies. No missing field is an approved zero, unlimited exposure or waiver.
- Review hosting/support commitments against actual staffing, monitoring, backup/restoration tests and vendor dependencies. No availability percentage, response time or recovery guarantee has been selected.
- Confirm data categories, roles, regions, subprocessors, safeguards, notice duties, export/retention and legal-hold handling. Draft a DPA, BAA or other sector-specific terms where applicable.
- Review payment scope and merchant/acquirer responsibilities; hosted checkout alone does not determine PCI scope. No funds custody or raw-card storage service is assumed.
- Reconcile insurance requirements and exclusions with the actual policies and broker advice.

## Research limits worth checking

The legal notes use dated official sources and identify retrieval limits. Wisconsin business-renewal statute Section 134.49 needs counsel verification of applicability, exclusions, disclosure mechanics, thresholds, reminder windows and notice methods before any automatic-renewal provision is adopted. The official Legislature page was not available in the research session; its numerical checks are explicitly flagged for verification.

Security incident notice under a contract is separate from an individual's statutory breach notice. Accessibility scope depends on the actual customer, including different public-sector requirements. Current federal negative-option rule status and any consumer or multistate obligations need a fresh check. This packet does not establish enforceability, compliance, coverage or legal sufficiency.

## Editing and execution controls

Blue bracketed fields in the legal Word files are editable plain-text content controls. They are not electronic signature fields. Repeated controls do not automatically synchronize: update every occurrence and search for remaining double brackets. Long scope, pricing, processing or retention values should become clearly identified attached schedules, not crowded inline text.

After counsel revisions, reconcile shared terms and every addendum, resolve open business decisions, attach immutable schedules, verify authority and obtain the owner's approval of the final version. Remove draft status only when authorized. Keep execution copies, client details, financial records and legal advice in restricted private storage. The public repository retains blank templates only.
