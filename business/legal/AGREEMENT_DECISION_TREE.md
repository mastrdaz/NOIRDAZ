# Agreement selection decision tree

DRAFT — ATTORNEY REVIEW — INTERNAL PREPARATION GUIDE

Start with the actual legal customer, service and data. A business name on a website is not enough to identify a contracting party. Do not promise scope or accept a customer contract before the appropriate review.

## 1 Are the parties only discussing confidential information

Use the Mutual NDA if confidential material needs to be exchanged before a services agreement. Complete its purpose, disclosure period, survival, return process and separate risk allocation. An NDA does not authorize development, payment, account access or production data processing.

If the discussions need only public information, an NDA may be unnecessary. Do not request sensitive data merely to complete sales discovery.

## 2 Is there already a signed MSA with this exact client entity

If no, use the MSA with a completed risk schedule and one appropriate project order. If yes, verify the signed version, current entity, scope and whether any prior amendment changes the standard terms. An affiliate, buyer of the client's business or differently named entity may require a new agreement or a consented assignment.

If the client requires its own form, route it to counsel. Compare precedence, IP, indemnity, data rights, security, audit rights, liability, renewal, exit and insurance before agreeing. Do not attach the NOIRDAZ MSA and assume it overrides the client's form.

## 3 Which order describes the work

- Website design/build: Website Build Agreement plus the MSA. Use it as the SOW; avoid a second generic SOW for the same scope.
- Custom application, integration, automation or implementation: generic SOW plus Software and Integration Addendum where applicable.
- Hosting only: generic SOW plus Managed Hosting Addendum and Support Policy if support is purchased.
- Maintenance or support only: generic SOW plus Support Policy and Software and Integration Addendum if software maintenance is in scope.
- Mixed engagement: one SOW may define build and recurring phases if each has separate deliverables, fees, start events and term; attach applicable addenda.

## 4 Are payments or financial workflows involved

Add the Payment Integration Addendum for checkout, processor integration, subscriptions, invoices connected to live payment events or payment-data handling. Add the software addendum for integration logic if needed. Document merchant of record, acquirer, card flow, token/log contents and the responsibility matrix.

Stop for specialist review if the model involves receiving or holding customer funds, splitting payouts as a platform, acting as payment facilitator or money transmitter, lending, stored value or direct handling of raw card data. A general technical-services contract does not resolve those roles.

## 5 Is personal or regulated data involved

Complete the Privacy and Data Questionnaire early. Convert verified facts into the signed processing/security and exit schedules. If applicable, obtain a separate DPA, BAA, international transfer terms or other sector-specific instrument before affected processing begins.

Stop for review if the service involves health information, financial-services customer records, children, education records, sensitive inference data, government information, biometric data or high-impact automated decisions. An ordinary confidentiality clause is not a substitute for the required agreement or operating controls.

## 6 Is this a hosted software product such as Sync Coverage

Use a project SOW for implementation work only if that is what is being sold. Separately define the subscription license, permitted users, product data rights, support, availability, renewal, cancellation, export and acceptable use in counsel-reviewed SaaS terms. The generic MSA does not grant a complete product license or consumer subscription regime merely because a product is listed in the catalog.

If individuals are buying for personal use, or the customer is a government body or school, stop and obtain the appropriate consumer or public-sector review. This suite is drafted around a business-to-business services relationship.

## 7 Is existing work changing or ready to go live

- Scope, price, timeline, integration, data category or operational commitment changes: signed Change Order before changed work or cost.
- Deliverable ready for review: acceptance section of the Project Acceptance and Launch Authorization, with test evidence and punch list.
- Production release: separate launch decision, exact version, environment, window, backup and rollback authority.
- Real payment transaction or new financial obligation: separate transaction-specific approvals; a contract, change order or launch form is not a blanket financial authorization.

## 8 Is service renewing or ending

For renewal, verify the actual signed term, applicable renewal rules, required notice method and deadline, and any changed prices. If no automatic-renewal clause was executed, get a signed extension. A recurring invoice schedule alone is insufficient to define renewal.

For exit, use the Offboarding Agreement and Transfer Checklist with the original MSA/SOW. Confirm scope ending, export and verification, domain/account ownership, final billing, access revocation, production deletion, backup expiration and holds. Use separate settlement language only if the parties intend a claim release.

## Minimum package examples

- Business brochure site: MSA + Website Build Agreement + acceptance/launch record; hosting/support addenda only if purchased
- Managed hosting subscription: MSA + SOW + hosting addendum + support exhibit + processing/retention schedules
- Internal business automation: MSA + SOW + software/integration addendum + acceptance/launch record
- Checkout build: MSA + website order or SOW + payment addendum + software addendum when needed + acceptance/launch record

In every example, first complete the data questionnaire, attachment index and owner/counsel approvals appropriate to the actual facts. The examples do not waive additional requirements.
