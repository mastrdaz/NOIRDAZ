## 1 Website scope

Provider will design and build the website described in [[WEBSITE_SPECIFICATION]]. The specification must identify page templates and maximum page count, navigation, languages, forms, CMS, integrations, content migration, search features, analytics and any commerce functionality. Included design concepts and revision rounds are [[DESIGN_REVIEW_ALLOWANCE]]. Deliverables and their allocated fees are [[DELIVERABLE_SCHEDULE]].

The supported devices, browsers and assistive-technology test environment are [[SUPPORTED_ENVIRONMENTS]]. Excluded services are [[EXCLUDED_SERVICES]]. The parties will not treat a reference website, mood board or demonstration as a promise to reproduce every feature. Functionality is included only when described in the agreed specification.

## 2 Content and design approval

Client will supply final text, lawful images, logo assets, product information and approvals according to [[CLIENT_DEPENDENCIES]]. Client remains responsible for the accuracy of its business claims, product descriptions and policies. Provider will implement agreed content faithfully and identify reasonably apparent omissions or inconsistencies; it does not act as Client's legal adviser.

Provider will submit design work at the checkpoints in [[DESIGN_APPROVAL_SCHEDULE]]. Client will provide consolidated feedback through [[CLIENT_PROJECT_CONTACT]]. Commercial changes require [[COMMERCIAL_APPROVERS]]. A request to revise approved work or add content beyond the allowance requires a change order; correction of Provider's failure to follow approved requirements does not consume a paid revision allowance.

## 3 Accessibility privacy and tracking

The technical accessibility target, test scope, manual checks, third-party exclusions and evidence to be delivered are [[ACCESSIBILITY_SPECIFICATION]]. Provider will perform those tasks and correct material deviations from that specification. A scan score, overlay or technical target is not a representation of universal legal compliance. The parties will identify public-sector, education or other special obligations before design approval and obtain qualified advice where needed.

Forms and analytics will collect only the data approved in [[DATA_PROCESSING_SCHEDULE]]. Client will supply or approve the privacy notices, cookie/consent choices, terms and other public-facing legal text before launch. Provider will not enable additional tracking, advertising pixels or session replay outside the approved inventory [[TRACKING_AND_FORM_INVENTORY]]. Neither Client approval nor third-party tool selection excuses Provider's own express data-security duties.

Payment functionality requires the Payment Integration Addendum and a documented card-data flow. The build fee alone does not authorize live transaction processing or collection of raw card details.

## 5 Review acceptance and launch

Acceptance tests are [[ACCEPTANCE_CRITERIA]], including agreed forms, links, responsive behavior, accessibility checks and migration checks. Provider will identify the delivered version and testing location. Client review, specific rejection, cure and retest follow MSA Section 5. Silence does not authorize publication.

Launch requires written authorization identifying the version, domain, date, approver, backup, rollback decision-maker and any accepted unresolved items in [[LAUNCH_PLAN]]. Provider will verify the readiness checks assigned to it, including certificate status, necessary access, backup and restoration path, and confirmation that production integrations are configured for the intended mode. Authorization to launch this website does not authorize unrelated DNS changes, account purchases, customer messages or payment charges.

The parties will complete the post-launch verification in [[POST_LAUNCH_CHECKS]]. Launch is not a waiver of the agreed warranty or outstanding punch-list work. The warranty period and remedies are those completed in the MSA. New content, third-party redesigns and features after acceptance are handled as maintenance or a change order.

Assigned Deliverables are [[ASSIGNED_DELIVERABLES]]. Background Materials are [[BACKGROUND_MATERIALS]]. Third-party components and licenses are [[THIRD_PARTY_COMPONENTS]]. The MSA governs assignment after allocated payment and the license needed to operate and maintain embedded Provider materials.
