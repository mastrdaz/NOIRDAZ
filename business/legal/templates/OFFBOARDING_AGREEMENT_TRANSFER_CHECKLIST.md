## 1 Services ending and continuing

The services ending are [[TERMINATING_SERVICES]], effective [[SERVICE_TERMINATION_DATE_AND_TIME]], including timezone. The termination basis and notice reference are [[TERMINATION_NOTICE_REFERENCE]]. Services continuing, their end dates and charges are [[CONTINUING_SERVICES]]. If none continue, that field must say “None.” A migration delay does not automatically renew service or extend billing.

The authorized receiving organization and individuals are [[AUTHORIZED_TRANSFER_RECIPIENTS]]. Provider and Client transition contacts are [[TRANSITION_CONTACTS]]. Client authorizes transfer only of the identified materials to those verified recipients through [[TRANSFER_METHOD]]. A new destination or recipient requires renewed written instructions before transfer.

## 2 Transfer scope and schedule

Provider will supply [[EXIT_DELIVERABLES]], identifying files, source, databases, schemas, media, configuration, DNS records, runbooks, licenses and export formats. The ordinary export included in the existing service is [[INCLUDED_EXIT_EXPORT]]. Additional migration labor, conversion or training is [[ADDITIONAL_EXIT_WORK]], at the authorized price or ceiling [[EXIT_WORK_FEES]] in [[CURRENCY]]. No unapproved third-party purchase or open-ended transition fee is authorized.

Milestones, owners, cutoff dates and the Client verification period are [[TRANSFER_SCHEDULE]]. Provider will protect transfer integrity and disclose known missing, corrupt or nonportable items. Client will verify receipt and practical usability in the receiving system before confirming completion. If a material defect is discovered, the parties will correct the transfer items assigned to Provider or agree a documented alternative.

## 3 Ownership accounts and dependencies

Client-owned domains and accounts remain Client's property. Provider will supply the records and delegated access needed for the authorized handoff and will not use them as leverage in a billing dispute. The domain, DNS, certificate, email and vendor-account transfer plan is [[ACCOUNT_AND_DOMAIN_TRANSFER_PLAN]]. Registrar restrictions, waiting periods and third-party approvals must be recorded rather than represented as within Provider's unilateral control.

Provider's Background Materials and third-party software remain subject to their applicable licenses. Assigned Deliverables and the embedded-material license follow the MSA's payment and ownership provisions. Nonportable licenses, tokens and subscriptions are [[NONPORTABLE_ITEMS]]. Provider will provide available documentation and cooperate in the approved replacement path without promising a vendor's consent.

## 4 Financial reconciliation

The final itemized reconciliation is [[FINAL_ACCOUNT_RECONCILIATION]], including earned service fees, approved noncancelable commitments, invoices paid, deposits applied, credits, refunds and disputed amounts. Undisputed amounts and refunds are due under [[FINAL_PAYMENT_AND_REFUND_DATES]]. Disputed items remain subject to the MSA process. Signature does not acknowledge an unresolved disputed debt or impose a new release.

The vendor renewal and billing-stop plan is [[VENDOR_CANCELLATION_PLAN]], identifying account owner, action owner, authorized cancellation date and evidence. Signing this document does not authorize moving funds, accepting a new agreement or making a new purchase. An identified account cancellation must still be carried out through its approved process and verified.

## 5 Security retention and deletion

Provider will maintain agreed safeguards during transition. Access will be revoked under [[ACCESS_REVOCATION_PLAN]] after the needed handoff, and Client will rotate shared credentials and assume recovery controls. Secrets must not appear in this checklist or its evidence links.

The export window closes [[EXPORT_WINDOW_END]]. Production data is scheduled for deletion [[PRODUCTION_DELETION_DATE]]. Backup copies expire under [[BACKUP_EXPIRATION_SCHEDULE]]. Any retained records and legal basis are [[RETAINED_RECORD_EXCEPTIONS]]. A legal hold must identify its custodian, record category, scope, review owner and release process without disclosing privileged advice. Held data remains restricted and may not be reused for other purposes.

Provider will confirm completed deletion and material exceptions in [[DELETION_CONFIRMATION_METHOD]]. Confirmation must distinguish deletion already performed from future backup expiration. Provider will not certify destruction it cannot verify. A hold delays only the affected deletion; it does not prevent otherwise authorized export or ordinary deletion of unrelated records.

Complete each item with an owner, date, evidence reference and exception, or mark it not applicable with a reason in [[TRANSFER_CHECKLIST_RECORD]].
