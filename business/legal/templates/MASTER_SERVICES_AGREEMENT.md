## 1 Agreement structure

1.1 This Agreement establishes the terms for services ordered in statements of work, including an executed website build order (each, SOW). Each SOW identifies its scope, deliverables, price, dependencies, schedule, acceptance criteria and applicable exhibits. Neither party must perform without an executed SOW. Estimates, demonstrations and proposals are not binding unless expressly incorporated into one.

1.2 The following order applies to a conflict: a later signed amendment identifying the provision changed; a signed data processing agreement, solely for its processing subject matter; this Agreement; service addenda; the SOW; and other incorporated exhibits. An SOW or addendum overrides this Agreement only by expressly identifying the section and agreed change. Purchase orders, portal terms and online policies do not amend this Agreement merely because a party receives, uploads or references them.

1.3 Each SOW is separately terminable. The end of one SOW does not terminate another. This Agreement remains in force while an SOW is active unless the parties agree otherwise.

## 2 Services and cooperation

2.1 Provider will perform the stated services with reasonable professional skill and care using personnel qualified for their assigned work. Provider controls its methods and personnel, subject to the specifications, security requirements and access limits agreed in writing. Provider may use subcontractors, remains responsible for their performance of its obligations, and will impose appropriate confidentiality, security and intellectual property obligations before granting access.

2.2 Each party will appoint an operational contact. Only the individuals identified as commercial approvers in the SOW may approve scope, fees or contractual changes. A technical contact may direct approved technical work but cannot expand a budget or waive contractual rights.

2.3 Client will timely provide the content, decisions, authorized access, licenses and dependencies assigned to it. Provider will identify reasonably apparent omissions and their likely effects. If a dependency is late, Provider will promptly propose a revised schedule and any cost impact. Additional charges require an approved change order; delay does not itself authorize new fees.

2.4 Provider will not knowingly introduce malicious code or obtain access beyond the authorized purpose. Client will not require unlawful use, unauthorized scraping, infringement, interference with third-party systems or access without the account holder's permission. Newly identified legal or security constraints require change control or termination of affected work.

## 3 Scope and changes

3.1 Services and deliverables are limited to the SOW and executed changes. Additional environments, revisions, integrations, content or regulatory work are changes when outside that scope.

3.2 A change request will state the requested work, reason, deliverables, fee impact, schedule, dependencies, testing and any privacy or security effect. Neither party is bound until its commercial approver signs the change order. Provider may continue unaffected work and pause the affected portion while approval is pending. If the parties cannot agree, existing scope controls unless infeasible; Section 13 then applies.

3.3 Incident containment authorized by the security provisions does not give Provider unlimited purchasing or billing authority. The SOW must identify any advance authorization and spending ceiling. Work or third-party expense above it requires further written approval, except that Provider may isolate an affected service as permitted by Section 12 without incurring unauthorized expenses.

4.1 Client will pay the fees, currency, invoice milestones and payment deadlines stated in each SOW. Deposits are credited as stated there. A deposit is not automatically nonrefundable. Provider will itemize services, approved expenses, applicable taxes and credits. Taxes legally imposed on Provider's net income, property or personnel remain Provider's responsibility. Client will provide valid exemption documentation when applicable.

4.2 Provider may incur reimbursable expenses or purchase third-party services on Client's behalf only within the specific written authorization and ceiling in the SOW or an approved change. The authorization must identify the service, payer, recurrence and material cancellation terms. A reimbursement allowance does not authorize Provider to accept unrelated terms for Client. Provider will disclose any markup.

4.3 Client will identify a good-faith invoice dispute, the disputed amount and its basis within [[INVOICE_DISPUTE_DAYS]] days after receipt, and pay undisputed amounts when due. The parties will promptly exchange supporting records and escalate unresolved disputes to their commercial approvers. Failure to identify a dispute within that period does not waive a claim that applicable law does not permit to be waived or an error not reasonably discoverable from the invoice. A timely disputed amount will not support suspension while Client cooperates in resolution.

4.4 Late charges, collection costs and payment-method surcharges apply only as expressly stated in [[LATE_CHARGE_TERMS]], to the extent permitted by applicable law. No blank field authorizes a fee. Provider will give the payment-default notice required by Section 12 before suspending for nonpayment. Credits and refunds will be applied or returned under [[REFUND_AND_CREDIT_TIMING]]. Neither party will use a billing dispute to disable Client's independent accounts or withhold Client-owned domains or Client Data contrary to Section 14.

4.5 Recurring charges start only upon the event identified in the SOW. A signed agreement for recurring services does not itself authorize debiting a bank account or card. Any such authority must be separately obtained through the designated payment system. Changes to recurring fees require the signed renewal or price-change process in the applicable SOW; posting a new price online does not change an existing order.
