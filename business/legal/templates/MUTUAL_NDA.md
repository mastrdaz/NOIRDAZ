## 1 Purpose and protected information

The parties wish to exchange information solely to evaluate and discuss [[NDA_PURPOSE]] (Purpose). Confidential Information includes nonpublic information disclosed for the Purpose in any form that is marked confidential or reasonably should be understood as confidential, including business plans, customer information, technical designs, source code, commercial terms and security details. The existence and status of discussions are Confidential Information if identified in [[NDA_DISCUSSION_CONFIDENTIALITY]].

Disclosures made before the Effective Date are covered only to the extent specifically identified in [[NDA_PRIOR_DISCLOSURES]]. This Agreement does not authorize sharing passwords, raw payment credentials, production personal data or other restricted information for which additional controls or agreements are needed.

## 2 Receiving Party obligations

The Receiving Party will use Confidential Information only for the Purpose, protect it with reasonable care no less protective than it uses for its own comparable information, and restrict access to its personnel and professional advisers who need it and are bound by protective obligations. It remains responsible for disclosures by those persons that would breach this Agreement if made by the Receiving Party, except where applicable law assigns a professional adviser an independent obligation and liability.

The Receiving Party will not disclose Confidential Information to an external AI or other third-party processing service without the Disclosing Party's written authorization identifying the data and service. It will not use the information to train a general-purpose model, develop an unrelated dataset or exploit the Disclosing Party's trade secrets outside the Purpose. Retaining something in memory does not exempt it from these duties.

No right to reverse engineer, decompile or bypass access controls is granted. Any evaluation involving those activities must be specifically authorized in a separate written scope, subject to rights that applicable law does not permit the parties to restrict.

## 3 Exclusions and required disclosure

The obligations do not apply to information the Receiving Party can demonstrate was lawfully known without restriction before disclosure, became public without its breach, was independently developed without use of Confidential Information, or was lawfully received from another source without a confidentiality duty. A combination is not excluded merely because individual elements are publicly known unless the relevant combination is also public or otherwise within an exclusion.

If disclosure is required by law, subpoena or court order, the Receiving Party may disclose only what is legally required. Where lawful, it will promptly notify the Disclosing Party, permit a reasonable opportunity to seek protection and cooperate at the Disclosing Party's expense. This Agreement does not prohibit lawful reporting to authorities, communications with counsel, or other activity protected from contractual restriction. Neither party must notify the other of a disclosure when law prohibits or protects against that notice requirement.

## 4 Ownership and absence of transaction commitment

All Confidential Information remains the property of the Disclosing Party or its licensors. Only the limited right to use it for the Purpose is granted. This Agreement creates no license to commercialize intellectual property, exclusivity, partnership, purchase commitment, employment relationship or duty to complete a transaction.

Information is supplied for evaluation without a warranty of completeness or accuracy, except that this sentence does not excuse fraud, intentional misrepresentation or another liability that cannot lawfully be excluded. Any service warranty or right to rely on specific information must be stated in a later signed agreement.

## 5 Duration return and retention

The permitted disclosure period ends [[NDA_DISCLOSURE_END_DATE]], unless extended in writing. Either party may end further exchanges by written notice. Confidentiality obligations continue for [[CONFIDENTIALITY_YEARS]] years after the disclosure period ends; trade secrets remain protected for as long as they qualify under applicable law, and personal information and retained copies remain protected while held.

On written request, the Receiving Party will stop the requested use and return or delete Confidential Information within [[NDA_RETURN_DAYS]] days, subject to the retained-record rules below. It will provide a written confirmation describing material exceptions upon request.

Copies may be retained only to meet a specific legal duty, legal hold or professional recordkeeping duty, or in ordinary backups pending scheduled expiration under [[NDA_BACKUP_EXPIRATION]]. They must remain protected, isolated from ordinary business use and deleted when the basis expires. A legal hold is limited to records genuinely within its scope. The Receiving Party will notify the Disclosing Party of a material retention exception where lawful without disclosing privileged legal advice.

## 6 Security events and remedies

The Receiving Party will promptly notify the Disclosing Party after discovering an unauthorized use or disclosure, take reasonable steps to limit it and cooperate in a lawful response. Notice will describe known facts and follow with material updates; it need not wait for a complete investigation.

A party may seek an injunction or other appropriate relief, but the court retains authority to determine the legal requirements and remedy. This Agreement does not predetermine irreparable harm, dispense with a bond where required or waive defenses. Liability caps, damages exclusions and their exceptions are [[NDA_RISK_ALLOCATION]]. No MSA cap is incorporated unless that field expressly identifies it and its exact application. Neither a blank field nor silence resolves the allocation of risk.
