# Discovery Questions

The goal is to understand the current workflow before recommending technology. Do not interrogate them; use these as a conversational checklist.

## Current payment workflow

1. What system generates the winning-bid invoice and winner email?
2. Is the invoice generated by the auction platform itself or another accounting/payment system?
3. What processor or virtual terminal do staff currently use for phone credit-card payments?
4. Do you already have online-payment capability somewhere that is simply not connected to the auction workflow?
5. How does staff currently mark an invoice as paid?
6. Does payment status feed back into the auction platform automatically or is it manual?
7. Approximately how many winning invoices/payments are handled:
   - per auction?
   - per week/month?
8. Roughly what percentage currently pay by:
   - phone card
   - card on file
   - PayPal
   - wire
   - mailed guaranteed funds
   - in-person payment
9. What is the typical invoice amount? What are the common high-dollar amounts?

## Invoice / bidder data

10. What identifiers exist for every transaction?
    - bidder number
    - auction ID
    - invoice ID
    - customer/account ID
11. Can the current system include those values in an email link?
12. Does the auction platform offer:
    - API access?
    - webhooks?
    - CSV export/import?
    - database/report export?
    - a way to mark an invoice paid?
13. Is there a test/sandbox account or demo environment?
14. Who owns/supports the auction software? Is it internally built or vendor supplied?

## Human-review exceptions

15. Which payments require staff involvement today?
16. What exactly changes for tax-exempt buyers?
17. If a tax exemption certificate is already on file, can the system know that?
18. If no exemption is on file, what information/documentation does staff need?
19. Are there special rules for:
    - invoices over $25,000?
    - wire transfers?
    - credit-card service fees?
    - debit cards?
    - international cards?
    - payment plans?
    - reseller/dealer accounts?
20. Are some auctions or sellers subject to different payment rules?

## Customer experience

21. Would you prefer:
    - one standard winner email with an online-pay option, or
    - separate email templates for eligible/not-eligible bidders?
22. Should most buyers be able to complete payment without contacting staff?
23. Should the phone number remain prominent for customers who prefer to call?
24. After payment, what should the customer see?
25. Should payment confirmation include seller/pickup contact instructions?
26. Is mobile use important? (Assume yes; confirm.)

## Staff workflow / reporting

27. How should staff be notified of successful payment?
    - email
    - dashboard
    - report
    - auction-system status
28. Who needs access to a payment dashboard?
29. What information should staff see?
30. Do you need refund/cancellation handling in the portal or can that remain in the processor?
31. What records need to be retained and for how long?
32. What would make the current process meaningfully easier for staff?

## Security / ownership

33. Would Wisconsin Surplus prefer its own Square/processor account? **Recommended: yes.**
34. Who should own:
    - processor account
    - domain/subdomain
    - payment records
    - auction credentials
35. Are there any existing IT/security requirements or vendors Noirdaz must coordinate with?

## Success criteria

36. If this works perfectly, what changes for your staff?
37. What would make you decide the project was not worth doing?
38. What is the most important outcome:
    - fewer calls
    - faster payment
    - less card handling
    - easier reconciliation
    - all of the above?
39. Is there a particular auction/date that would make a good pilot?
